# Ashtakavarga follows Parasara and the book's worked answers

Ch. 12 defines ashtakavarga by eight tables (19–26) and two reductions, and states exact bands for reading the counts. Unlike chapters 10 and 11, it prints full answers to check against: every BAV and SAV of four charts, a whole SoAV and 21 pindas. Its text leaves three gaps in ekaadhipatya sodhana and one table cell contradicts its own answers. hora computes everything the chapter defines, follows Parasara where §12.8 names a dispute, and lets the worked answers settle the gaps.

## Evidence

A throwaway implementation of the decisions below ran on hora's positions with Tables 19–26 as printed. Each table's total is the classical one (48, 49, 39, 54, 56, 52, 39; lagna 49).

- **Reproduced, every cell:** Exercises 19 and 20 (chart 6, at both its 12:49 pm at 5:17 East and chart 11's 1:08 pm IST, which give the same rasis); Exercise 22's BAV and SAV (chart 7); Example 39's SAV of chart 3's rasi chart and D-10; Exercise 21's SAV of chart 12's D-10; Table 27's PAV and Exercise 18's lists (chart 6); Examples 37, 38, 40, 41 and 43; §25.6's Examples 111 and 112 (charts 62 and 24, pp. 339–340).
- **Varahamihira:** each combination of §12.8's three variants adds 2–6 wrong cells on chart 6.
- **Exercise 19's "5\*"** (the Moon in Pi, p. 163) has no footnote; 5 fits the row's total of 49 and Pi's SAV of 30.
- **The lagna occupies:** in Exercise 22, chart 7's lagna is alone in Sc, and Ar holds the Moon, Saturn and Rahu. Counting the lagna, both rasis are occupied and nothing changes, which gives the printed SoAV. Counting only grahas leaves three cells wrong (Sun, Moon and Saturn in Sc).
- **Equal values in rule (3):** chart 7's Jupiter has 2 in Sg (occupied) and 2 in Pi (empty) after trikona sodhana; the book prints Pi 0. It is the only such cell.
- **Rahu and Ketu:** undecided. In chart 7 they share rasis with planets; chart 6 prints only Mercury's SoAV, unaffected.
- **Table 28's Virgo:** it prints 6 (p. 159). With 6, six of Exercise 22's seven rasi pindas are too high by exactly their SoAV in Vi (Sun 155 for 152, Mercury 99 for 95, Saturn 166 for 162); Venus's is 0 there. With 5, all 21 pindas match. Example 43 has 0 in Vi, so it cannot tell.

## Decision

Where the book is explicit, hora follows it. Where it is silent or contradicts itself, these readings apply:

- **What is computed:** PAV and BAV of the seven planets and the lagna (Tables 19–26); SAV, the seven planets' BAVs summed, the lagna's not included (§12.4); and for the seven planets, SoAV, rasi pinda, graha pinda and sodhya pinda (§12.7). The lagna's BAV is shown though the book never uses it, as it defines Table 26.
- **References:** the seven planets and the lagna, houses counted as whole rasis from the reference's rasi (§12.8, ADR 0010). Rahu and Ketu take no part.
- **Parasara, not Varahamihira:** Tables 19–26 as printed, with no option. The knowledge base names §12.8's three disputed cells.
- **Trikona sodhana:** rule (1) as the book has it, not footnote 44's "some authors" who zero the third rasi when two are zero. Rules (1) and (2) are cases of (3).
- **Ekaadhipatya sodhana:** "occupied" is by any of the eight references, so the lagna occupies (Exercise 22). Rahu and Ketu do not, as they are no reference and an unqualified "planet" means the seven (ADR 0014); the book does not decide this. In rule (3), an empty rasi whose value equals the occupied one's becomes 0, as lower does (Exercise 22's Jupiter in Pi, and rule (4a)).
- **Table 28's Virgo multiplier is 5, not the printed 6**, an erratum shown by Exercise 22's pindas. The golden values stay as printed.
- **Bands:** a planet's BAV count of 5–8 is favourable, 4 neutral and 0–3 unfavourable (§12.3); an SAV count of 30 or more is strong, 25–29 average and under 25 weak (§12.4), as "30 or more" comes first. The lagna's BAV has no band, as §12.3 speaks of a planet. Bands are shown in words, never by colour alone, and nothing beyond them is judged.
- **Which charts:** the full set for the rasi chart and the varga shown (§12.5), each from its own lagna and positions, ignoring the page's reference point. Pindas apply in a varga too, since §12.5 names them.
- **Not computed:** transits, muhurta and footnote 45's Vaastu. The knowledge base explains them.

## Considered options

- **Varahamihira, or a setting for him:** the book argues Parasara's form resists corruption and its answers follow him; a setting would offer what it rejects.
- **Only grahas occupy in ekaadhipatya sodhana:** the literal "planet", but Exercise 22 contradicts it.
- **Rahu and Ketu occupy:** plausible, as they are in a rasi, but they are not among ashtakavarga's references and no chart needs them.
- **An equal value stays in rule (3):** the literal text, but Exercise 22 prints 0.
- **Table 28 as printed:** every rasi pinda of Exercise 22 with SoAV in Vi would fail its golden value.
- **No lagna BAV:** smaller, but leaves out a table the book defines.
- **No bands, as ADR 0013 and 0014 give no verdict:** those chapters gave no measure; this one states exact numbers.
