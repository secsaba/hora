# Avasthas follow the book's worked Sayanaadi states, and strength stays the book's simple rules

Ch. 15 (pp. 187–206) describes shadbala, ashtakavarga bala and vimsopaka bala without computing them (§15.2–15.3). It defines four families of avasthas (states) of a graha (§15.4): by age (Table 35), alertness, attitude and mood, and activity, the Sayanaadi avasthas (Tables 36–37). It also gives the stronger co-lord and stronger rasi (§15.5), which hora already computes (ADR 0012). The activity state needs the ghati running at birth and the first sound of the native's name. That sound is the only birth input the book asks for beyond date, time and place. The mood rules are loose ("joined closely by Sun", Mudita defined twice). Earlier ADRs left the sthira karakas and the paaka lagna's co-lord for this chapter (ADR 0003, ADR 0011). hora computes every avastha family and the sthira karakas, reads the loose rules as below, and leaves the balas to the knowledge base.

## Evidence

hora's positions and its own sunrise, read against the book's two worked cases:

- **Example 49** (4 April 1970, 17:50 IST, 81E12, 16N15, sound 1; pp. 199–200): hora's sunrise is 05:58:40, so 711 minutes have passed and G = 30, as the book's "6 am" gives. Any sunrise from about 05:50 to 06:14 gives the same G. The lagna is in Virgo, the Moon in Poorvabhadrapada and Mercury, Jupiter and Venus at 3Ar08, 9Li46 and 7Ar55, as printed. Mercury is Gamana and cheshta, Jupiter Nriyalipsaa and drishti, Venus Sabhaa and cheshta, as printed.
- **Exercise 24** (8 November 1927, 08:30 LMT, 67E03, 24N52, sound "L", 5; pp. 201, 205): the book prints no positions. With hora's sunrise of 06:11 LMT, G = 6, and the Sun is Sabhaa and cheshta, Mars Nidraa and drishti, Jupiter Aagama and cheshta, as the answer says. A 6 am sunrise gives G = 7, and all three answers would fail. G moves all three indices together, so the answer fixes the book's sunrise between about 06:06 and 06:30.
- **Remainder 0:** Exercise 24's Mars has remainder 0 and is Nidraa, index 12.
- **The nodes:** neither case uses Rahu or Ketu, so their index and the A of a node are readings, not checked.

## Decision

Where the book is explicit, hora follows it. Where it is silent or contradicts itself, these readings apply:

- **What is computed:** all four avastha families, and the sthira karakas. Shadbala, ashtakavarga bala and vimsopaka bala are in the knowledge base only. The book calls shadbala's computation "beyond the scope of this book", its definitions differ between programs (§15.3), and the book works no values to check against.
- **Which chart:** the rasi chart only, counted from the lagna whatever the reference point. Age states need degrees, which a varga has none of (ADR 0009). The other families follow so that one table holds them all.
- **Relationships:** a friend's, neutral's or enemy's rasi is read from the compound relationship with the rasi's seven-planet lord, as the glossary and the yogas' "friend's rasi" read it (ADR 0014). The mood rungs follow it: adhimitra is Mudita, mitra Saanta, sama Deena, satru or adhisatru Duhkhita. Swapna is mitra, adhimitra or sama, and Sushupta satru or adhisatru. Enemies who conjoin or aspect a graha (Kshudhita, Trishita, Kshobhita) are by the compound relationship too. The nodes have none (ADR 0017).
- **The nodes' states:** only those read from a rasi or from company: Jaagrita, Sushupta when debilitated, Deepta, Swastha, Garvita, Vikala, Khala, Kopita, Lajjita, and Trishita, read as a watery rasi with no natural benefic's aspect, since a node has no enemy to aspect it. The relationship states do not apply, and their cells read "—".
- **Mood readings:** "malefic" is `natural_nature`'s, as in the yogas (ADR 0014). Aspects are rasi drishti, as §14.3 and §15.5 use. Kopita is a graha in the Sun's rasi, with "closely" to judge: hora has no combustion orbs, and ADR 0014 treats "combust" the same way. Lajjita is a graha in the 5th from the lagna joined by any of the Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu or Ketu. Trishita is a graha in Cancer, Scorpio or Pisces, aspected by an enemy and by no natural benefic. Khala holds in a malefic's own rasi too, as the book words it. Garvita's moolatrikona and Deepta's exaltation are whole signs, as ADR 0014 reads Go. Mudita holds by either of its two definitions. No graha joins himself (ADR 0012), so the Sun is never Kopita, Kshobhita or Lajjita by himself. A graha lists every mood state that holds, in the book's order: the nine first, then the six.
- **Sayanaadi avastha (§15.4.4):** (C × P × A + M + G + L) mod 12, a remainder of 0 being Nidraa (12). P is 8 for Rahu and 9 for Ketu, in Parasara's order. The book gives their strength adjustment (4) but not their index. A is the 3°20′ part of the rasi counted from its start, for the nodes too, as the navamsa counts. G is the ghati running since the Vedic day's sunrise, so a night birth counts from the sunrise before it. Without a Vedic day there is no Sayanaadi avastha, and the page says so, as it does for the yogadas.
- **The name's sound (Table 37):** a choice of the five groups as the book prints them, or "not given", validated server-side to 1–5 and carried in the URL like the other birth fields. No name is asked for or stored. Hungarian spellings (cs, sz, gy) are not mapped to a group: that would be interpretation. The knowledge base says to choose by how the name is pronounced. Without a sound, the avastha is shown and its strength (cheshta, drishti or vicheshta) is not, with the reason.
- **Results:** the chart page shows the states and the activity strength only. The knowledge base gives each graha's Sayanaadi results and Parasara's special results (§15.4.4), as the classics ascribe them, harsh ones included, in our own words (ADR 0004, ADR 0014).
- **Sthira karakas (§8.3):** the father is the stronger of the Sun and Venus, and the mother the stronger of the Moon and Mars, by `stronger_planet` (§15.5.1's rules 1–4 and 5b, ADR 0012). Footnote 26's day/night rule is in the knowledge base only. The page lists all seven with the relatives §8.3 gives them. Jupiter's husband is labelled "in a woman's chart" and Venus's wife "in a man's chart", since hora does not ask the native's sex.
- **A tie between chara karakas** leaves the next karakatwa empty for good. §8.2 says to use "the corresponding sthira karaka" without saying which one corresponds to which karakatwa. Only the PiK and MK pair clearly, and advancement is ranked in whole arcseconds (ADR 0011), so a tie almost never happens.
- **The paaka lagna** of a Scorpio or Aquarius lagna keeps the seven-planet lord (ADR 0003). Ch. 15 gives the stronger co-lord only for arudha padas and rasi dasas (§15.5.1).
- **Not yet:** §15.5.1's rule 5a (dasa length) waits for the dasas (ADR 0012). §15.5.2's warning about ayur and AK-based dasas waits for them too, and is noted in the knowledge base.
- **Golden values:** Example 49's printed positions in `hora-swe`; both cases' activity states and strengths in `hora-chart`, from hora's own sunrise (`docs/book-charts.md`). §15.4's examples without a birth (Table 35's four degrees) get ordinary tests.

## Considered options

- **The name as free text, mapped to a sound:** Table 37's transliteration does not cover Hungarian spelling, and the server would receive a personal name.
- **No sound at all:** drops the strength the book's examples end with.
- **Combustion orbs for Kopita:** the book gives none, and ADR 0014 already declines to invent them.
- **Mood states only in the knowledge base:** the rules are loose, but each can be read with the choices above, and the book lists them as states to judge with.
- **The natural relationship for friend, neutral and enemy:** contradicts the glossary's "friend" and ADR 0014, and with the compound relationship for "good friend" it would make one graha both Mudita and Saanta.
- **Filling a tied karakatwa with a sthira karaka:** the book does not say which one corresponds.
- **Shadbala from BPHS:** outside the book, which declines it and has no values to check against.
