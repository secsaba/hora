# The calculation choices hora offers, and their defaults

A chart depends on choices the book makes once for all its examples but other schools make differently. hora lets the user pick each one; the default is whatever reproduces the book's example charts, since those are the golden tests.

## Decision

- **Calendar:** Gregorian (default) or Julian. The user states it, since countries switched at different dates.
- **Ayanamsa:** Lahiri (default), True Chitra, Raman, True Pushya, True Mula. The book names its ayanamsa "Chitrapaksha/Lahiri" (ch. 1, §1.3.13), and its printed example charts (charts 1–4 and 10, to the arcminute) match the library's Lahiri: every graha within a minute (chart 10, from 1542, within 100″), where True Chitra is 1–1.8′ ahead throughout. True Chitra was the default until the golden tests were found to pin Jagannatha Hora's values, which match True Chitra, rather than the book's.
- **Node type:** mean (default) or true.
- **Chart style:** South Indian (default), North Indian, East Indian: the three of ch. 1, §1.3.4. A display choice only; it never changes a calculation.
- **Degrees in a chakra:** only the lagna and the grahas carry them, and only in the rasi chakra (a varga chakra has none, ADR 0009). HL, GL and the arudha padas are marked by rasi alone; the Special lagnas table gives HL's and GL's degrees. A display choice, like chart style: it keeps a full area readable.
- **Reference point:** the lagna (default; the book counts bhavas from it when no reference point is stated, ch. 1, §1.3.3), any graha, the hora, ghati or sree lagna, which the book counts bhavas from (ch. 7 §7.3.7–7.3.8, ch. 20), paaka lagna, the rasi of the lagna's lord (ch. 7 §7.3.5), the atma karaka (ch. 7 §7.3.6, ch. 8 §8.2), or the arudha lagna or upapada lagna (ch. 9 §9.3–9.4). The other bhava arudhas are not offered: the book seldom counts from them. The bhaava lagna is not offered: the book defines it only for completeness (ch. 5, footnote 10). A display choice only, like chart style.
- **Sunrise definition:** upper limb, apparent (default; the book recommends the first visible ray, ch. 5, p. 48), or centre/upper limb with true/apparent position: the four options of the author's software, Jagannatha Hora.
- **Lunar month:** amanta only, from one new Moon to the next, as the book defines it (ch. 1, §1.3.8.2); purnimanta months (full Moon to full Moon) are not offered. Of two months started in the same rasi the first is Adhika and the second Nija, the common rule, since the book does not say.
- **Rahu's and Ketu's dignities:** the book's only (ch. 3, Table 6): Rahu exalted in Gemini, Ketu in Sagittarius. Schools that exalt them in Taurus and Scorpio are not offered.
- **Upagrahas:** Jagannatha Hora's defaults only: Gulika rises at the beginning of Saturn's part and Maandi at its middle, Kaala, Mrityu, Arthaprahaara and Yamaghantaka at the middle of their parts, Vyatipaata is 360° − Dhuma, and day and night start at sunrise and sunset. Ch. 4 §4.3 prints Gulika and Maandi the other way round, but the book's own Example 142 (Chart 85, "Gulika within 1º from lagna", ch. 36, p. 478) fits only Gulika at the beginning, so §4.3 is taken as a misprint. Footnote 9's "beginning of each part" and other Dhuma–Vyatipaata relations are not offered.
- **Special lagnas:** the hora, ghati and bhaava lagnas advance from the Sun's longitude at the Vedic day's sunrise at fixed clock rates, 30° an hour, 30° per 24 minutes and 15° an hour (ch. 5 §5.2–5.4), over the exact time since that sunrise. They do not use hours scaled to the day's length, and the time is not rounded to the minute. A birth before sunrise counts from the previous day's sunrise (Exercise 8). Without a Vedic day only the sree lagna exists. The book's printed hora and ghati lagnas fit no sunrise definition (ADR 0008).
- **Varga chakras:** the twenty of ch. 6 §6.2, by the book's rules only; the other versions of D-2, D-3, D-8 and D-11 that §6.2 and §6.7 mention are not offered. D-2 puts the Sun's hora in Leo and the Moon's in Cancer: §6.2.2 names only the horas, and Example 27 (Jupiter in the Moon's hora, D-2 in Cancer) fixes the rasis. A body's place in a varga is a rasi (ADR 0009).
- **Varga chakra shown:** any of the twenty beside the rasi chakra, D-9 by default (the most used after the rasi chart, §6.2.9). A display choice only, like chart style; the North Indian drawing counts bhavas from the reference point's rasi in that varga, since a varga chakra is read as a chart of its own (§6.1).
- **Paaka lagna in a varga chakra:** counted in that chart, since it "applies to all divisional charts" (ch. 7 §7.3.5): the lord of the lagna's rasi in that varga, in its rasi in that varga, not the rasi chart's lagna lord carried over.
- **Paaka lagna for a Scorpio or Aquarius lagna:** the seven-planet lord, Mars or Saturn, as the Rasi lord column has it (#123). Ch. 15 §15.5.1 names the stronger of the co-lords (Ketu, Rahu) only for arudha padas and rasi dasas, and §7.3.5 says only the lagna's lord, so arudha padas changed nothing here; ch. 15 decides whether this line changes. The Rasi lord column keeps the seven-planet lord too.
- **Chara karakas:** the book's eight, Rahu included and measured back from his rasi's end, ranked in whole arcseconds; no seven-karaka scheme (ADR 0011).
- **Atma karaka as a reference point:** counted like a graha, from its rasi in each chart: the rasi chakra from its rasi, a varga chakra from its rasi in that varga, which in D-9 is the karakamsa (ch. 7 §7.3.6). Karakamsa is not carried into the rasi chart or other vargas; the book reads it only in the navamsa.
- **Sthira karakas:** not computed until ch. 15 decides "the stronger" of the Sun and Venus (father) and of the Moon and Mars (mother) (ch. 8 §8.3, footnote 26); footnote 26's day/night rule is not offered. A tie between chara karakas leaves the next karakatwa empty until then (ADR 0011).
- **Arudha padas:** the twelve bhava arudhas and nine graha arudhas of ch. 9 §9.2 and §9.5, in any varga chakra, by the book's steps only, with the stronger lord and stronger rasi of ch. 15 §15.5 (ADR 0012).
- **Arudha lagna or upapada lagna as a reference point:** counted in each chart from that chart's own arudha pada, since the book reads "the 10th house from AL in D-10" (ch. 9 §9.3): the rasi chakra from the rasi chart's, a varga chakra from that varga's. Nothing is carried over between charts.
- **Aspects:** graha drishti and rasi drishti of ch. 10 §10.2–10.3, on whole rasis, in any varga chakra, with no orb or strength. Rahu and Ketu have rasi drishti only (Exercise 14, ADR 0013).
- **Argalas:** the argalas and virodhargalas of ch. 10 §10.5–10.6 on every rasi, in any varga chakra, caused only by the nine grahas. hora gives no verdict on obstruction, does not label subhaargala or paapaargala, and does not compute "several malefics in the 3rd" (ADR 0013).
- **Yogas:** 154 of ch. 11's 156 entries in the rasi chart and the varga shown, from the lagna, as present, to judge or absent, with no rating and no strength (ADR 0014).
- **A lord in a yoga:** either co-lord of Scorpio or Aquarius, unless one is in the rasi, when the other is its main lord, and a §11.6 rule also tries a seven-planet lord at home (ADR 0014). The stronger lord stays with arudha padas, and the seven-planet lord with the paaka lagna and the Rasi lord column.
- **Mercury's nature in a yoga:** by his company in that chart, a tie evaluating the yoga both ways, so that a difference is to judge (ADR 0014). Subhaargala and paapaargala stay unlabelled (ADR 0013).
- **Closeness of a yoga's pair:** the difference of the two degrees within their rasis, for a conjunction, an aspect or a parivartana, in the rasi chart only (ADR 0014).
