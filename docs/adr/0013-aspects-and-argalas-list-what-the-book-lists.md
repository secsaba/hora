# Aspects and argalas list what the book lists, with no verdict

Ch. 10 gives exact rules for which rasis and grahas a graha aspects and for which grahas cause argala or virodhargala on a rasi. It then leaves gaps: "several" malefics in the 3rd has no number, §10.7's tie is settled by "compare the strengths" with no rule, and an argala's nature rests on a benefic or malefic reading that §3.2.2 leaves open for Mercury. hora computes and shows the lists, and does not judge beyond them.

## Evidence

- A throwaway implementation of the decisions below, on hora's positions, reproduced every row of Exercise 16 (chart 5, D-9, Aquarius counted in reverse), Exercise 15's rasis, Examples 35 and 36 on Saturn (charts 6 and 7), and Exercise 17's argalas on Saturn (chart 8). It also reproduced the argalas stated for chart 12's lagna and chart 17's A3 (both D-10), chart 32's Cancer (D-10), chart 27's Libra (D-16, as chart 33), chart 7's Sagittarius (as chart 34), chart 38's Libra, chart 67's Pisces and chart 59's Leo (both D-9, the latter as chart 83). Exercise 14, and the grahas in Exercise 15, were checked by hand against chart 5's D-9 rasis.
- Exercise 16 lists Mars and Saturn, 3rd from Virgo, as virodhargala, so two malefics are not "several" in the book's own table.
- Example 35 calls three grahas' argala on Saturn decisive and does not mention Rahu in the paired 3rd rasi. The book does not apply §10.7's count as a mechanical rule.
- Exercise 14 leaves out Rahu and Ketu, and Exercise 15 includes them. The later node aspects checked by hand fit rasi drishti: "Rahu's rasi aspect on AL" (chart 20, p. 221) and Rahu from Le on Cp, which is not the 7th (chart 57, p. 325). Rahu from Sg on chart 81's Ge lagna (p. 466) is both the 7th and rasi drishti, so it doesn't decide the question.

## Decision

Where the book is explicit, hora follows it. Where it is silent or contradicts itself, these readings apply:

- **Rahu and Ketu:** rasi drishti only, as Exercise 14 has it, despite §10.2's "all planets". They do cause and obstruct argala, as in Exercise 16.
- **What is aspected, and what causes argala:** only the nine grahas count, never a lagna, a special lagna or an arudha pada (Exercises 14–16; ADR 0012). A house causing argala (§10.5) stays an explanation in the knowledge base.
- **An argala's target is a rasi:** argala on a graha, GL or A3 is argala on its rasi in that chart (§10.6's note: "on Vi and on the planets in Vi"). Choosing that point as the reference point makes it the first row.
- **Ketu's reversal:** by the target rasi holding Ketu in that chart. Ketu causing argala on another rasi changes nothing (Exercise 16, Scorpio).
- **Several malefics in the 3rd:** not computed. The table lists whoever is in the 3rd as virodhargala, and the page and the knowledge base state the principle.
- **Subhaargala and paapaargala:** not labelled. Mercury's company has no stated chart or tie rule, and no value computed here depends on it. Chapter 11 decides if its yogas need a resolver.
- **No verdict:** hora shows each argala beside its obstruction and does not mark one as obstructed, dominant or unobstructed.
- **No orb, no strength of aspect:** both drishtis fall on whole rasis in any varga chakra (ADR 0009, ADR 0010).

## Considered options

- **Three or more malefics count as "several":** the only number that agrees with Exercise 16, but an inference that no value in the book tests. A wrong threshold would silently move grahas from one side to the other.
- **Infer Mercury's nature from the chart at hand, a tie staying benefic:** a plausible reading, but it would add a new rule to display two words the reader can work out.
- **A computed verdict by §10.7's count:** the book doesn't say whether to count per pair or overall, and its tie needs strengths that have no rule here. A verdict would look more certain than the book is.
- **Graha drishti for the nodes on the 7th:** follows §10.2's first sentence, but contradicts Exercise 14's answer, and none of the book's node aspects checked needs it.
