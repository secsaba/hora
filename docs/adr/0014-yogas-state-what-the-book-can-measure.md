# Yogas state what the book's rules can measure, and leave the rest to judge

Ch. 11 lists 156 yogas: 98 named and 58 numbered. It warns that software listing yogas is "sometimes erroneous", requires participants to "be strong" (§11.6) with no threshold anywhere in the book, and has gaps and misprints. Later chapters state only about a dozen of them on a chart, so most rules can never be checked against a chart of the book. hora computes them anyway, marks what it cannot measure, and gives no rating.

## Evidence

A throwaway implementation of the readings below ran on hora's positions for the 82 pinned chart casts, rasi chart unless stated. It covered the Ravi, Chandra (without Adhi) and Mahaapurusha yogas, the Naabhasa yogas without Maalaa, Sarpa, Vajra and Yava, the basic Raaja yoga, Vipareeta and Kalpadruma.

- **Reproduced:** Maalavya, Sasa, Ruchaka and both raja pairs of chart 10; Bhadra of charts 6 and 11; Vesi of charts 17, 19 and 20; Kalpadruma of chart 9; Vipareeta of chart 6; the raja yoga of charts 13 and 14; in D-20 chart 13's Moon and Mars and its Budha-Aaditya; in D-7 chart 18's Rahu and Venus; in D-10 chart 90's Moon and Jupiter; AK with PK in chart 3's lagna.
- **Closeness reproduced:** chart 10's Venus and Saturn 0.69° (the book "less than a degree"), chart 14's Sun and Jupiter 8.4° ("more than 8°"), chart 6's Mars and Sun 17′ ("17'"), and chart 10's Moon and Mercury 1.3° from a close aspect, 178.7° by longitude.
- **Amsas reproduced:** chart 14's Sun and Jupiter (5, Simhaasanaamsa), chart 13's Jupiter (3, Uttamaamsa), chart 10's Mars (5, Simhaasanaamsa), Moon and Mercury (0).
- **Not reproduced, so not golden:** chart 10's Venus and Saturn are printed as Uttamaamsa and Paarijaataamsa, and chart 6's Mercury as Uttamamsa (pp. 137, 230). hora counts 2 for each of the three, so Paarijaataamsa, and no reading of the vargas found gives 3. As with ADR 0008, these are cited and the cause stays open.
- **Prevalence:** the basic Raaja yoga is on 70 of the 82, Vipareeta present on 45 and to judge on 22. Mercury's company ties on 12. The book expects the first ("in many charts", §11.7.2).
- **Co-lords:** chart 18 (D-7) makes Rahu the 5th lord because Saturn is in Aquarius, chart 10 names Saturn with Rahu in Aquarius, chart 6 names Mars and chart 20 names Ketu for Scorpio with neither co-lord in it. Only the reading below fits all four: the seven-planet lord fails chart 18 and the stronger lord fails chart 20.

## Decision

Where the book is explicit, hora follows it. Where it is silent or contradicts itself, these readings apply:

- **What is computed:** 154 entries, every §11.2–11.10 entry except Raaja (18), which only says when others work better, and Daridra (10), whose "benefic houses" the chapter leaves undefined (§12.2's are ashtakavarga's, per planet). The book's own "alternately" forms and Brahma's second form count, as does the Dhana basic rule (Moon, Mercury, Jupiter or Venus exalted in the 2nd).
- **Knowledge base only:** "some authors" variants (Gaja-Kesari from lagna, the simplified Kalpadruma, "9th lord" in Kaahala), §11.3's general guidelines, kartari on any house, and Raaja (6)'s looser form. A later statement that a graha gives a yoga "from" a dasa lagna or an adopted lagna is not reproduced (see the charts below).
- **Three states:** present, to judge, or absent. A condition with no measure in the book is to judge and named in words. Absent yogas are not listed.
- **Strength:** never computed. A "strong" condition is to judge wherever a rule states one. §11.6's general requirement that participants be strong stays in the knowledge base, or every yoga would be to judge.
- **Partial yogas:** not listed if a checkable condition fails, despite §11.11. Raaja (1) is the exception: AK with PK stands like the lagna and 5th lords conjoined, each a raja yoga by itself (chart 3, p. 265), so it is listed when either holds and says which.
- **A rasi's lord in a yoga:** Scorpio and Aquarius have two lords, either of which can form the yoga; if one is in the rasi, the other alone is its lord, as Parasara says on p. 218. If both are, either counts. The row names the graha. This is not the stronger lord, and the paaka lagna and the Rasi lord column keep the seven-planet one.
- **Rahu and Ketu:** an unqualified "planet" or "all the planets" means the seven planets. In particular they form no yoga from the 2nd and 12th of the Sun or Moon, and do not count in any of Kemadruma's checks. "Malefic" includes them (§3.2.2). They count where a rule names them or as a lord.
- **Mercury's nature:** by his company in the chart being read, the Moon classed by paksha and the nodes as malefics. §3.2.2 has no tie, so on a tie the yoga is evaluated both ways: agreement is the result, and a difference is to judge. If both ways give the yoga with different grahas, the saumya reading's stand, listing Mercury. ADR 0013's argala labels stay as they are.
- **"Occupied by":** not exclusive unless the rule says "only" (Amala, Parvata), per Maalaa's note. Named houses (Vajra, Yava, Matsya, Koorma, Bheri) each hold at least one. A set of places (Hari, Hara, Brahma, Vasumati) holds every natural benefic and at least one, leaving out the graha counted from, as in §11.3's third guideline. Adhi needs any two or more natural benefics in its places, in any arrangement, leaving out the Moon counted from. A Mercury made kroora by his company is not one of them, and his own yogas, such as Bhadra, stand apart (decided on #220). Vasumati's "full results" conditions are to judge.
- **Aakriti shapes:** all seven planets are in the listed places and every place is occupied. Kamala and Vaapi say only "in", so being within them is enough. A chart may show more than one.
- **Sankhya:** only if no other Naabhasa yoga applies (§11.5.4).
- **Deep exaltation and combustion exist only in the rasi chart.** Vidyut and Jaya are rasi-only, need the uchcha rasi and leave the deep degree to judge. Where "not combust" is a stated condition (Gaja-Kesari, Raaja (11)), in the rasi chart it is to judge only if the graha shares the Sun's rasi; in a varga it does not apply. Budha-Aaditya's combustion is a note on its power (§11.2.4), so it stays in the knowledge base.
- **Maraka:** §14.2's 2nd and 7th lords and the malefics conjoining or aspecting with graha drishti those houses or lords, without weighing "powerfully", in the chart being read. §11.10's "also become malefics" is read as marakas.
- **Vipareeta:** present if a dusthana lord is in a dusthana. Two dusthana lords conjoined elsewhere are to judge (strength, p. 494), which keeps chart 6's and chart 90's statements together. Each lord in a dusthana and each conjoined pair is a row of its own.
- **Misprints:** a rule wins over its example (Adhi's benefics fall in the 4th and 5th from the Moon). Hamsa's "Ruchaka" is read as Hamsa. The Pisces Dhana entry's first half, the Moon in both the 5th and 11th, is impossible and not computed; its second half is, and the knowledge base says why.
- **Which charts:** the rasi chart and the varga shown, counted from the lagna whatever the reference point. Rasi-only because they mix charts or need the rasi: Kalpadruma, Vishnu, Gouri, Chandikaa, Bhaarathi, Vidyut, Jaya, Raaja (7) and (9), Daridra (8) and (9).
- **Magnitude (§11.7.2):** Raaja rows show closeness and each graha's Dasa Varga amsa, without a rating. Afflictions wait for ch. 13's Table 30. Closeness, for a conjunction, an aspect or a parivartana alike, is the difference of the two degrees within their rasis, in the rasi chart only (ADR 0009). The nodes' degrees are read as they stand, not from the rasi's end.
- **Results:** on the chart page only the yoga and how it forms, with a link to a list of what was checked against the book's charts. The knowledge base lists each yoga's results as the classics ascribe them, harsh ones included (Sakata's ugly nails, say), with nothing softened, modernised or left out. The wording is our own only because the book's text is copyrighted (ADR 0004). §11.11's caveats stay on the overview page, as the book's own.

## Considered options

- **Only the classes with exact rules:** the safest, but it drops the Raaja yogas that later chapters use most.
- **A threshold for "strong" (amsabala, say):** would look more certain than the book, as ADR 0013 found for argalas.
- **Ignoring "strong":** would overstate Kaahala, Sankha and the like.
- **The stronger lord, or the seven-planet lord, in yogas:** each fails a statement above.
- **A tie leaves Mercury benefic:** simple, but adds a rule the book lacks, and ties are common because Mercury stays near the Sun.
- **Listing partial yogas:** nearly every chart would show dozens.
- **Inferring Saturn for Pisces Dhana's 11th from the other entries:** plausible, but not what is printed.
- **Results on the chart page:** would put a list of claims in every row, close to the book's copyrighted text, where the page's job is how each yoga forms.
- **Softening or modernising the results:** would misstate what the classics say.
