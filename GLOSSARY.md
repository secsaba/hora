# hora

Vedic (Jyotish) astrology as taught in P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach*, which is the authority on language. Sanskrit terms are spelled as in the book; where it gives alternatives, the first spelling in its defining table wins. The Sanskrit term is canonical in both languages, in the book's spelling; the book's English word (`_Gloss_`) or the settled Hungarian one (`_Hungarian gloss_`) may follow only in parentheses, e.g. "bhava (house)", "bhava (ház)".

## Language

**Jyotish**:
Vedic astrology, the "science of light". A Jyotishi is its practitioner.
_Hungarian_: Jyotish
_Avoid_: Hindu astrology, Indian astrology

**Graha**:
A body with considerable influence on living beings on Earth: the seven planets Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn, and the two chaayaa grahas, Rahu and Ketu.
_Hungarian_: graha
_Hungarian gloss_: bolygó
_Gloss_: planet
_Avoid_: Uranus, Neptune and Pluto as grahas (no seer taught them)

**Chaayaa graha**:
A shadow graha: a mathematical point, not a real planet. Rahu and Ketu are the two.
_Hungarian_: chaayaa graha
_Hungarian gloss_: árnyékbolygó
_Gloss_: shadow planet

**Rahu**:
The Moon's north node, a chaayaa graha.
_Hungarian_: Rahu

**Ketu**:
The Moon's south node, a chaayaa graha, always exactly opposite Rahu.
_Hungarian_: Ketu

**Node type**:
Whether Rahu and Ketu are placed at the mean or the true lunar node.
_Hungarian_: holdcsomó típusa

**Upagraha**:
One of eleven moving mathematical points, placed in a chart beside the grahas but not grahas themselves.
_Hungarian_: upagraha
_Gloss_: sub-planet

**Sun-based upagraha**:
One of the five upagrahas placed at a fixed distance from the Sun's longitude: Dhuma, Vyatipaata, Parivesha, Indrachaapa and Upaketu.
_Hungarian_: Nap-alapú upagraha
_Avoid_: Vyatipata, Indrachapa

**Time-based upagraha**:
One of the six upagrahas that is the lagna at a moment in a part of the day or night: Kaala, Mrityu, Arthaprahaara, Yamaghantaka, Gulika and Maandi.
_Hungarian_: időalapú upagraha
_Avoid_: other upagraha; Kala, Artha Praharaka, Artha Prahara, Yama Ghantaka, Mandi

**Retrograde**:
A graha's apparent backward motion along the zodiac, as seen from the Earth. The Sun and Moon never move backward; the mean nodes always do; the true nodes mostly do, but at times move forward.
_Hungarian_: retrográd

**Conjunction**:
Two grahas at exactly the same longitude; loosely, two grahas in the same rasi.
_Hungarian_: együttállás

## Zodiac

**Zodiac**:
The 360° circle along which longitudes are measured from a starting point. Vedic astrology uses the nirayana (sidereal, fixed) zodiac; the sayana (tropical, moving) zodiac starts at the vernal equinox, which drifts against the stars.
_Hungarian_: zodiákus
_Avoid_: Sky (that is the Sky term below)

**Longitude**:
A position on the zodiac, from 0° to just under 360°, as seen from the Earth. Its Sanskrit name is sphuta.
_Hungarian_: hosszúság
_Avoid_: Position, degree

**Ayanamsa**:
The angle between the sayana and the nirayana zodiac at a given moment; subtracting it from a tropical longitude gives the sidereal one. Schools disagree on its value because they disagree on which star starts the zodiac.
_Hungarian_: ayanamsa
_Avoid_: Ayanamsha, precession

**Rasi**:
One of the twelve 30° parts of the nirayana zodiac, Aries (Mesha) to Pisces (Meena).
_Hungarian_: rasi
_Hungarian gloss_: jegy
_Gloss_: sign
_Avoid_: Rashi, zodiac sign

**Vishama rasi**:
An odd rasi, counted from Aries: Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius; also oja rasi, and male. The other six are sama (also yugma) rasis, even and female.
_Hungarian_: vishama rasi, sama rasi
_Hungarian gloss_: páratlan jegy, páros jegy
_Gloss_: odd sign, even sign
_Avoid_: Male and female sign as names

**Vishamapada rasi**:
An odd-footed rasi: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius; also ojapada rasi. The other six are samapada (also yugmapada) rasis, even-footed.
_Hungarian_: vishamapada rasi, samapada rasi
_Hungarian gloss_: páratlan lábú jegy, páros lábú jegy
_Gloss_: odd-footed sign, even-footed sign

**Chara, sthira, dwiswabhava**:
The three kinds of rasi by how they move, in turn from Aries: chara (movable, dynamic), sthira (fixed, stable) and dwiswabhava (dual, now one, now the other).
_Hungarian_: chara, sthira, dwiswabhava
_Hungarian gloss_: mozgó, állandó, kettős
_Gloss_: movable, fixed, dual
_Avoid_: Cardinal, mutable, modality as a name

**Element**:
One of the five of which Hindu philosophy holds everything is made: agni (fire), bhoo (earth), vaayu (air) and jala (water), each held by three rasis in turn from Aries, and aakaasa (ether), present in every rasi. Each element is also ruled by a graha: agni by Mars (the Sun shares its nature), bhoo by Mercury, vaayu by Saturn, aakaasa by Jupiter, jala by Venus (the Moon shares its nature). The book also calls an element a tattva.
_Hungarian_: elem
_Hungarian gloss_: tűz, föld, levegő, víz, éter
_Gloss_: fire, earth, air, water, ether

**Pitta, vaata, kapha**:
The three natures of Ayurveda, each a mix of elements: pitta (bilious, fire and water) transforms, vaata (windy, air and ether) moves, kapha (phlegmatic, earth and water) binds. Fiery rasis are pitta, earthy ones vaata, watery ones kapha, airy ones mixed.
_Hungarian_: pitta, vaata, kapha
_Gloss_: bilious, windy, phlegmatic

**Guna**:
One of the three qualities everything has: sattwa (purity), rajas (energy, passion) and tamas (darkness). Each rasi and each of the seven planets has one; a rasi or graha with sattwa is saattwik, with rajas raajasik, with tamas taamasik.
_Hungarian_: guna
_Hungarian gloss_: minőség (sattwa: tisztaság, rajas: energia, tamas: sötétség)
_Gloss_: quality
_Avoid_: Sattva, rajo guna, tamo guna as names

**Divaa rasi**:
A rasi strong in the daytime; the other six are nishaa rasis, strong at night. Of the two rasis a graha owns, one is divaa and one nishaa.
_Hungarian_: divaa rasi, nishaa rasi
_Hungarian gloss_: nappali jegy, éjszakai jegy
_Gloss_: day sign, night sign

**Seershodaya rasi**:
A rasi that rises head first; a prishthodaya rasi rises with its feet first (literally its back). Pisces rises both ways and has no name of its own.
_Hungarian_: seershodaya rasi, prishthodaya rasi
_Hungarian gloss_: fejjel kelő jegy, lábbal kelő jegy
_Gloss_: head-rising sign, feet-rising sign
_Avoid_: Ubhayodaya (not the book's term)

**Varna**:
One of the four classes of Hindu society, each shown by the rasis of one element: Brahmanas (scholars, watery), Kshatriyas (warriors, fiery), Vaisyas (traders, earthy), Sudras (workers, airy). Each of the seven planets has one too; read as a basic nature, not a family's caste.
_Hungarian_: varna
_Hungarian gloss_: rend (társadalmi rend)
_Gloss_: class
_Avoid_: Caste

**Nakshatra**:
One of the twenty-seven 13°20′ parts of the nirayana zodiac, Aswini to Revati. Names are spelled as in the book's Table 2, which writes long vowels doubled (Swaati for Svātī); the knowledge base also gives the common spelling, here where it differs: Aswini (Ashvini), Mrigasira (Mrigashira), Aardra (Ardra), Pushyami (Pushya), Aasresha (Ashlesha), Makha (Magha), Poorva Phalguni (Purva Phalguni), Swaati (Svati), Visaakha (Vishakha), Anooraadha (Anuradha), Moola (Mula), Poorvaashaadha (Purva Ashadha), Uttaraashaadha (Uttara Ashadha), Sravanam (Shravana), Dhanishtha (Dhanishta), Satabhishak (Shatabhisha), Poorvaabhaadra (Purva Bhadrapada), Uttaraabhaadra (Uttara Bhadrapada). The common spellings are the main names in Wikipedia's *List of Nakshatras*, without diacritics.
_Hungarian_: nakshatra
_Hungarian gloss_: holdház
_Gloss_: constellation
_Avoid_: Lunar mansion, asterism

**Pada**:
One of the four 3°20′ quarters of a nakshatra.
_Hungarian_: pada
_Hungarian gloss_: negyed
_Gloss_: quarter

**Abhijit**:
A twenty-eighth nakshatra, the last quarter of Uttaraashaadha, counted only in special charts such as the Kota and Sarvatobhadra chakras.
_Hungarian_: Abhijit

**Vimsottari lord**:
The graha that rules a nakshatra in the Vimsottari dasa.
_Hungarian_: Vimsottari ura

**Lagna**:
The sidereal longitude of the eastern horizon's intersection with the ecliptic at the moment and place of birth.
_Hungarian_: lagna
_Hungarian gloss_: aszcendens
_Gloss_: ascendant ("Asc" in a North Indian chart)
_Avoid_: Rising sign

**Special lagna**:
A mathematical point other than the lagna used as a lagna, such as the hora lagna.
_Hungarian_: különleges lagna
_Gloss_: special ascendant

**Hora lagna**:
The special lagna that starts at the Sun's longitude at the sunrise of the Vedic day and advances one rasi per hour of clock time. It shows the self as seen through wealth.
_Hungarian_: hora lagna
_Gloss_: HL
_Avoid_: Horalagna

**Ghati lagna**:
The special lagna that starts at the Sun's longitude at the sunrise of the Vedic day and advances one rasi per ghati (24 minutes of clock time). It shows the self as seen through power and fame.
_Hungarian_: ghati lagna
_Gloss_: GL
_Avoid_: Ghatika lagna, ghatilagna

**Sree lagna**:
The special lagna reached by adding to the lagna the fraction of its nakshatra the Moon has covered, taken as that fraction of the whole zodiac. It shows prosperity.
_Hungarian_: sree lagna
_Gloss_: SL
_Avoid_: Shree lagna, Sri lagna

**Bhaava lagna**:
The special lagna that starts at the Sun's longitude at the sunrise of the Vedic day and advances one rasi per two hours of clock time. The book defines it for completeness and does not use it.
_Hungarian_: bhaava lagna
_Gloss_: BL
_Avoid_: Bhava lagna, bhavalagna (bhava is the house)

## Chart

**Sky**:
What the ephemeris gives for one moment and place: the grahas' sidereal longitudes and speeds, the lagna, the ayanamsa and the Vedic day. A chart is prepared from it.
_Hungarian_: égbolt
_Avoid_: Positions (it holds more than positions), chart data

**Chart**:
Every graha, upagraha, lagna and special lagna placed in the rasi it occupies, usually drawn as twelve boxes. Its Sanskrit name is chakra.
_Hungarian_: chakra
_Avoid_: Horoscope, kundli, képlet

**Rasi chart**:
The chart made from the longitudes themselves, undivided.
_Hungarian_: rasi chakra
_Avoid_: Natal chart, D-1 (as a name)

**Varga chakra**:
A chart made by dividing each rasi into n equal parts and mapping each part to a rasi again; read as an independent chart for one area of life.
_Hungarian_: varga chakra
_Hungarian gloss_: osztott chakra
_Gloss_: divisional chart
_Avoid_: Harmonic chart

**Bhava**:
A rasi counted from a reference point: the rasi holding it is the 1st bhava, the next the 2nd, and so on to the 12th. Without a stated reference point, the reference is the lagna.
_Hungarian_: bhava
_Hungarian gloss_: ház
_Gloss_: house

**Reference point**:
The graha, lagna or special lagna from which bhavas are counted.
_Hungarian_: viszonyítási pont

**Chart style**:
How a chart is drawn: South Indian or East Indian, where each rasi has a fixed place (rasi-based), or North Indian, where each bhava has a fixed place (bhava-based).
_Hungarian_: chakra rajzmódja

## Grahas

**Rasi lord**:
The graha that owns a rasi: Mars owns Aries and Scorpio, Venus Taurus and Libra, Mercury Gemini and Virgo, the Moon Cancer, the Sun Leo, Jupiter Sagittarius and Pisces, Saturn Capricorn and Aquarius. Rahu co-owns Aquarius and Ketu Scorpio; plain "lord" means the seven-planet one.
_Hungarian_: jegyúr
_Avoid_: Ruler, sign ruler

**Saumya graha**:
A natural benefic: Jupiter, Venus, Mercury when alone or with more natural benefics, and the Moon in Sukla paksha. A kroora graha is a natural malefic: the Sun, Mars, Saturn (named in ch. 13 §13.2, missing from §3.2.2), Rahu, Ketu, Mercury when joined by more natural malefics, and the Moon in Krishna paksha.
_Hungarian_: saumya graha, kroora graha
_Hungarian gloss_: jótékony bolygó, ártó bolygó
_Gloss_: natural benefic, natural malefic
_Avoid_: Subha graha, paapa graha (the book's second spellings)

**Avatara**:
An incarnation of Vishnu; each of the nine came from one graha, as each rasi is one of Vishnu's limbs.
_Hungarian_: avatara
_Hungarian gloss_: megtestesülés
_Gloss_: incarnation

**Sapta dhaatu**:
The seven matters the human body is made of (bones, blood, marrow, skin, fat, semen, muscles), each ruled by one of the seven planets.
_Hungarian_: sapta dhaatu
_Gloss_: seven matters
_Avoid_: Dhatu (that is the dhatu of dhatu, moola, jeeva)

**Dhatu, moola, jeeva**:
The three kinds of things the nine grahas rule: dhatu (metals and materials), moola (roots and vegetables) and jeeva (living beings).
_Hungarian_: dhatu, moola, jeeva
_Gloss_: metal, root, living being

**Ayana**:
Half of a year by the Sun's course: Uttara ayana while the Sun moves from Capricorn to Gemini, Dakshina ayana from Cancer to Sagittarius.
_Hungarian_: ayana
_Hungarian gloss_: északi út, déli út
_Gloss_: north and south course

**Ritu**:
One of the six two-month seasons of a year, vasanta (spring) to sisira, each ruled by a graha.
_Hungarian_: ritu
_Hungarian gloss_: évszak
_Gloss_: season

**Digbala**:
A graha's strength in one direction: Mercury and Jupiter in the east (the lagna), the Sun and Mars in the south (the 10th bhava), Saturn in the west (the 7th), the Moon and Venus in the north (the 4th).
_Hungarian_: digbala
_Hungarian gloss_: irányerő
_Gloss_: directional strength

## Dignities and relationships

**Dignity**:
Whether the rasi a graha occupies is its uchcha, moolatrikona, own rasi or neecha; in any other rasi a graha has no dignity. Separate from the graha's compound relationship with that rasi's lord, which a graha in a dignity rasi also has.
_Hungarian_: méltóság

**Uchcha**:
The rasi where a graha is exalted, most excited; its deep exaltation point is one degree within it. The opposite rasi, where it is debilitated, is its neecha, with the deep debilitation point at the same degree.
_Hungarian_: uchcha, neecha
_Hungarian gloss_: emelt, esésben
_Gloss_: exaltation, debilitation
_Avoid_: Fall

**Moolatrikona**:
The rasi where a graha does its formal duty, strong and duty-minded. For most grahas it is one of their own rasis, and in it a span of degrees gives moolatrikona results and the rest those of uchcha or own rasi.
_Hungarian_: moolatrikona

**Own rasi**:
A rasi a graha owns, where it is most natural and at ease. The book gives no Sanskrit term.
_Hungarian_: saját jegy
_Avoid_: Swakshetra, sign of rulership

**Natural relationship**:
Whether a graha is a friend (mitra), neutral (sama) or enemy (satru) of another by nature, the same in every chart: counted from its moolatrikona. Only the seven planets have one.
_Hungarian_: természetes viszony
_Avoid_: Permanent relationship, naisargika

**Tatkaala relationship**:
Whether a graha is a temporary friend or enemy of another in one chart: friends occupy the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 10th, 11th and 12th rasis from it.
_Hungarian_: tatkaala viszony
_Gloss_: temporary relationship

**Compound relationship**:
The natural and tatkaala relationships combined into one of five: adhimitra (good friend), mitra (friend), sama (neutral), satru (enemy), adhisatru (bad enemy). A graha in a rasi whose lord is its mitra or adhimitra is in a friendly house, satru or adhisatru an inimical house; plain "friend" in a chart means this.
_Hungarian_: összetett viszony
_Hungarian gloss_: jó barát, barát, semleges, ellenség, nagy ellenség
_Gloss_: good friend, friend, neutral, enemy, bad enemy
_Avoid_: Bitter enemy, great friend

## Calendar and panchaanga

**Solar month**:
The time the Sun takes to move 30°; a solar year is its 360° and a solar day its 1°.
_Hungarian_: szoláris hónap

**Tithi**:
The time in which the Moon's longitude gains 12° on the Sun's; thirty make a lunar month.
_Hungarian_: tithi
_Hungarian gloss_: holdnap
_Gloss_: lunar day

**Paksha**:
Half of a lunar month: Sukla paksha, the waxing half when the Moon is 0°–180° ahead of the Sun, and Krishna paksha, the waning half.
_Hungarian_: paksha
_Hungarian gloss_: félhónap
_Gloss_: fortnight

**Lunar month**:
The time from one Sun-Moon conjunction to the next, named by the rasi of the conjunction that starts it (Chaitra for Pisces, and so on); months reckoned so, ending at the new moon, are amanta. Its Sanskrit name is maasa. When two consecutive months start in the same rasi, the first, in which the Sun enters no new rasi, is Adhika (extra) and the second Nija (real); the book does not say which is which, so this follows the common rule.
_Hungarian_: holdhónap

**Sun-Moon yoga**:
One of 27 parts of the sum of the Sun's and the Moon's longitudes, each 13°20′ long, Vishkambha to Vaidhriti.
_Hungarian_: Nap–Hold yoga
_Avoid_: Yoga (that is a combination of grahas), nitya yoga

**Karana**:
Half of a tithi; there are eleven, seven repeating and four fixed.
_Hungarian_: karana

**Vaara**:
The weekday, Sunday to Saturday, each ruled by a graha.
_Hungarian_: vaara
_Hungarian gloss_: a hét napja
_Gloss_: weekday

**Vedic day**:
The day from one sunrise to the next. A birth before sunrise belongs to the previous civil date's Vedic day. Near the poles a birth has none when the sunrise before it or the one after it is missing (polar night, midnight sun).
_Hungarian_: védikus nap
_Avoid_: Day (ambiguous with the civil date, the solar day and the tithi)

**Sunrise definition**:
Which point of the Sun's disc must be on the horizon (centre or upper limb), and whether that is the true or the apparent position (refraction ignored or included).
_Hungarian_: napkelte-meghatározás
_Avoid_: Hindu rising (the library's Hindu rising is the centre, true and seen from the Earth's centre)

**Part (of the day or night)**:
One of the eight equal parts of the day (sunrise to sunset) or of the night (sunset to the next sunrise) of a Vedic day; a birth exactly at sunset is in the night. Each part is ruled by a graha, except one lord-less part.
_Hungarian_: nyolcad (a nappal vagy az éjszaka nyolcada)
_Avoid_: portion, hora

**Hora**:
One of the 24 equal parts of a Vedic day. The first is ruled by the vaara's graha, the rest in the order Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon; that graha is the hora lord.
_Hungarian_: hora
_Avoid_: Hour, planetary hour

**Panchaanga**:
The five limbs of a moment: tithi, vaara, nakshatra, Sun-Moon yoga and karana; also an almanac listing them.
_Hungarian_: panchaanga

**Muhurta**:
A time chosen for starting a venture, with an auspicious panchaanga.
_Hungarian_: muhurta
_Gloss_: electional astrology

## Dasas

**Dasa**:
A period of life ruled by a graha or a rasi under one dasa system. Its sub-periods are antardasas, theirs pratyantardasas; a top-level dasa is also called a mahadasa.
_Hungarian_: dasa
_Hungarian gloss_: időszak
_Gloss_: period
_Avoid_: Dasha, MD/AD/PD outside tables

**Dasa system**:
A rule for dividing a life into dasas and reading results from them. A nakshatra dasa starts from the Moon's nakshatra, a rasi dasa from rasis; an ayur dasa shows longevity, a phalita dasa general results.
_Hungarian_: dasarendszer

## Birth data

**Birth data**:
The birth date, birth time, UTC offset and birth place from which a chart is prepared. It never includes a name.
_Hungarian_: születési adatok
_Avoid_: Birth details

**Birth time**:
The local clock time of birth, as recorded.
_Hungarian_: születési idő
_Avoid_: Birth moment

**Birth place**:
The geographic latitude and longitude (Hungarian: földrajzi szélesség és hosszúság) and the altitude of the place of birth. Plain longitude always means the zodiac one.
_Hungarian_: születési hely

**Calendar**:
Whether a birth date is given in the Gregorian or the Julian calendar.
_Hungarian_: naptár

**UTC offset**:
How far the birth time is ahead of UT: a stated amount, or derived from the birth place when the birth time is in local mean time or local apparent time.
_Hungarian_: UTC-eltérés
_Avoid_: Time zone (a zone is only a way to find the offset)

**Local mean time**:
Clock time set by the mean Sun at the birth place's longitude: UT plus four minutes per degree east. Many births before standard time were recorded in it.
_Hungarian_: helyi középidő
_Avoid_: LMT, local time

**Local apparent time**:
Clock time set by the true Sun at the birth place, as a sundial shows it: local mean time plus the equation of time, which varies over the year by up to about 16 minutes. Some old records, especially Indian ones, give it.
_Hungarian_: helyi valódi idő
_Avoid_: LAT, sundial time
