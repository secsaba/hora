# Sodhya pinda

A planet's [BAV](/en/kb/ashtakavarga#bav) goes through two reductions, trikona sodhana and then ekaadhipatya sodhana, and what is left is its **sodhita ashtakavarga** (SoAV), the reduced ashtakavarga (§12.7). Weighting the SoAV by fixed multipliers gives two sums, the rasi pinda and the graha pinda, and together they make the planet's sodhya pinda. Only the seven planets have them: the lagna's BAV is not reduced.

## Trikona sodhana {#trikona-sodhana}

The first reduction, the trinal one, works on the four sets of rasis in [trikona](/en/kb/bhava-categories#trikona) (trine) to each other: Aries, Leo and Sagittarius; Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn; Gemini, Libra and Aquarius; Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces (§12.7.1). In each set, the lowest of the three counts is taken from all three.

The book states it as three rules: if a rasi of the set has 0, nothing changes; if all three are equal, all become 0; otherwise take the lowest from each. The first two are only the third in special cases, as the book itself says. It also mentions some authors who, when two rasis of a set have 0, make the third 0 as well; the book rejects that as against the rule's spirit, and hora does not use it (ADR 0015).

## Ekaadhipatya sodhana {#ekaadhipatya-sodhana}

The second reduction, the co-owned one, works on the five pairs of rasis with the same lord: Aries and Scorpio (Mars), Taurus and Libra (Venus), Gemini and Virgo (Mercury), Sagittarius and Pisces (Jupiter), Capricorn and Aquarius (Saturn) (§12.7.2). Cancer and Leo each have a lord of their own and are left as they are. On each pair, after trikona sodhana:

1. If either rasi has 0, nothing changes.
2. If both rasis are occupied, nothing changes.
3. If one is occupied and the other empty: an empty rasi with less than the occupied one becomes 0, and one with more is brought down to the occupied one's count.
4. If both are empty: equal counts both become 0, and otherwise the higher is brought down to the lower.

### What occupies a rasi {#occupied}

The book says a rasi is occupied by "a planet". hora counts any of ashtakavarga's [eight references](/en/kb/ashtakavarga#references), so the lagna occupies the rasi it is in even with no planet there: [chart 7]({{chart:7}}&topic=ashtakavarga)'s answers in Exercise 22 come out right only that way. Rahu and Ketu do not occupy a rasi, as they are no reference and an unqualified "planet" means the seven. The book does not decide this case, and none of its charts depends on it (ADR 0015).

### Equal counts under rule (3) {#equal}

Rule (3) says what happens when the empty rasi has less or more than the occupied one, but not when they are equal. hora treats equal as less: the empty rasi becomes 0, just as two equal empty rasis do under rule (4). Exercise 22 prints it so, for Jupiter in [chart 7]({{chart:7}}&topic=ashtakavarga) (ADR 0015).

## A worked case {#worked-case}

A made-up chart: the lagna alone in Scorpio; the Sun and Mercury in Gemini; the Moon in Taurus; Venus in Cancer; Mars in Capricorn; Jupiter and Saturn in Virgo, with Ketu; Rahu alone in Pisces. Aries, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius and Aquarius are empty. Its Mars has this BAV, totalling 39 as every Mars BAV does; the numbers are made up to show the rules, not cast from the chart.

| Rasi | BAV | After trikona sodhana | SoAV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aries | 4 | 2 | 0 |
| Taurus | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| Gemini | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Cancer | 4 | 0 | 0 |
| Leo | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| Virgo | 4 | 1 | 1 |
| Libra | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Scorpio | 6 | 2 | 2 |
| Sagittarius | 3 | 1 | 0 |
| Capricorn | 4 | 1 | 1 |
| Aquarius | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| Pisces | 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Trikona sodhana.** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius have 4, 2 and 3: the lowest is 2, leaving 2, 0 and 1. Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn have 3, 4 and 4, leaving 0, 1 and 1. Gemini, Libra and Aquarius are untouched, as Libra has 0. Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces have 4, 6 and 5, leaving 0, 2 and 1.

**Ekaadhipatya sodhana.**

- **Aries and Scorpio, 2 and 2.** The lagna alone makes Scorpio occupied, and Aries is empty. The counts are equal, so Aries becomes 0 under rule (3). Were the lagna not counted, both would be empty and both would become 0.
- **Taurus and Libra, 0 and 0.** Rule (1): nothing changes.
- **Gemini and Virgo, 2 and 1.** Both occupied, rule (2): nothing changes.
- **Sagittarius and Pisces, 1 and 1.** Rahu does not occupy Pisces, so both are empty and equal, and both become 0 under rule (4). Were Rahu counted, only Sagittarius would.
- **Capricorn and Aquarius, 1 and 2.** Mars occupies Capricorn, and the empty Aquarius has more, so it is brought down to 1.

## The pindas {#pindas}

**Rasi pinda:** multiply each rasi's SoAV by that rasi's multiplier, its rasimana, and add up the twelve products.

{{table:rasimana}}

**Graha pinda:** for each of the seven planets, multiply the SoAV in the rasi it occupies by that planet's multiplier, its grahamana, and add up the seven products. A planet in a rasi with 0 adds nothing.

{{table:grahamana}}

**Sodhya pinda:** the rasi pinda plus the graha pinda (§12.7.3).

In the worked case, the rasi pinda is 2 × 8 for Gemini, 1 × 5 for Virgo, 2 × 8 for Scorpio, 1 × 5 for Capricorn and 1 × 11 for Aquarius: 53. The graha pinda is 2 × 5 each for the Sun and Mercury in Gemini, 1 × 8 for Mars in Capricorn, and 1 × 10 for Jupiter and 1 × 5 for Saturn in Virgo: 43. The Moon and Venus stand in rasis with 0. So Mars's sodhya pinda is 96.

### Virgo's multiplier {#virgo}

The book's table of rasi multipliers prints 6 for Virgo; hora uses 5. With 6, every rasi pinda in Exercise 22 ([chart 7]({{chart:7}}&topic=ashtakavarga)) whose SoAV has a count in Virgo comes out too high by exactly that count; with 5, all 21 pindas of the exercise match. hora takes the 6 for an erratum (ADR 0015). In the worked case, Virgo's 6 would make the rasi pinda 54 and the sodhya pinda 97.

## What the pindas are for {#use}

The book calls the pindas very important for predicting key events, and returns to them in its part on transit analysis (§25.6): there a planet's sodhya pinda, together with the rekhas it has in a house, points to a nakshatra and a rasi whose transits by other planets mark that house's events. The book also calls this open to research, as the [Varahamihira dispute](/en/kb/ashtakavarga#parasara) and differing versions of the reductions change the pindas. hora computes the pindas but does no timing.
