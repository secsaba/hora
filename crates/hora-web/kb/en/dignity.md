# Dignity

A [graha](/en/kb/graha) (planet) does not act the same in every [rasi](/en/kb/rasi) (sign). In most rasis it is simply a guest. In a few it has a dignity: a rasi that suits it in a particular way and colours how it behaves. There are four: its own rasi, its moolatrikona, its uchcha (exalted) rasi and its neecha (debilitated) rasi. The first three make a graha strong, each in its own mood; the fourth weakens it.

## Own rasi {#own-rasi}

Each of the seven planets owns one or two rasis and is their rasi lord (the graha that owns a rasi): the Sun owns Leo, the Moon Cancer, and each of the other five owns two. In its own rasi a graha is at ease and acts by its own nature, without strain.

Rahu and Ketu are given an own rasi too, Aquarius and Scorpio, which they share with Saturn and Mars. That counts for the nodes' dignity only: where hora names a rasi's lord, it names Saturn for Aquarius and Mars for Scorpio.

## Moolatrikona {#moolatrikona}

Each graha has one moolatrikona rasi. For most it is one of their own rasis; the Moon, Rahu and Ketu have theirs elsewhere. In its moolatrikona a graha is strong and steady, acting with purpose rather than for its own comfort.

## Uchcha {#uchcha}

In its uchcha rasi a graha is exalted: eager, keen and at its most enthusiastic. One degree in that rasi is its deep exaltation point, where the effect is at its height.

## Neecha {#neecha}

The rasi opposite its uchcha, the 7th from it, is a graha's neecha rasi, where it is debilitated: uneasy, working against its grain. Its deep debilitation point is at the same degree as the deep exaltation point.

In any other rasi a graha has no dignity.

## Degree splits

Where a graha's moolatrikona rasi is also its own or its uchcha rasi, the rasi is shared out by degree, and only part of it gives moolatrikona results. Each span below includes its first degree but not its last: the Sun at exactly 20° of Leo is in his own rasi, not his moolatrikona. hora decides to the whole arcsecond. Rahu's and Ketu's moolatrikona rasis are not split.

## Rahu and Ketu

hora gives Rahu and Ketu the dignities the book gives them: Rahu exalted in Gemini, Ketu in Sagittarius, with no deep points. Other schools differ, some exalting Rahu in Taurus and Ketu in Scorpio, and hora does not offer their choices.

## Dignity and friendship

A graha's dignity depends only on the rasi it occupies. How it gets on with the lord of that rasi is a separate matter, described under [graha relationships](/en/kb/graha-relationships): a graha in its neecha rasi still has a relationship with that rasi's lord, and both are read.
