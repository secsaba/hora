# Arudha padas

A [bhava](/en/kb/bhava) (house) shows a matter of life as it really is. An arudha pada is the [rasi](/en/kb/rasi) (sign) that shows how that matter appears. This page explains the difference, how the arudha padas of bhavas and of [grahas](/en/kb/graha) (planets) are found, how to read them, and the strength rules they depend on.

## Reality and appearance {#maya}

The lagna (ascendant) stands for who one really is, but other people seldom meet that person. They judge by what they can see. A violinist who plays beautifully in a small town may stay unknown, while a less gifted colleague fills concert halls and is taken for the better musician. The invitations go to the famous one, whatever an expert's ear might say.

Maya (illusion) is the name for that gap: what a thing seems to be, as against what it is. It is not a flaw to be cured but the ordinary condition of worldly life, and it decides many worldly questions. A question about one's inner life needs the reality; a question about rank or reputation needs the appearance. An astrologer has to keep the two apart, and arudha padas are the tool for it. They come in two kinds: bhava arudhas show how the world sees one, and graha arudhas show how one sees the world.

## Bhava arudhas {#bhava-arudha}

A bhava arudha is the arudha pada of a bhava. Every varga chakra (divisional chart) has its own twelve, always counted from the lagna of that chart. To find one:

1. Take the rasi of the bhava.
2. Find that rasi's lord and the rasi the lord stands in. Scorpio and Aquarius have two lords, and the [stronger lord](#stronger-lord) acts.
3. Count from the bhava's rasi to the lord's rasi, in zodiac order, with the starting rasi as 1.
4. Count the same number onward from the lord's rasi.
5. If you land on the bhava's own rasi or the 7th from it, go on to the 10th from where you landed. This happens exactly when the lord is in the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th from the bhava.

So the bhava arudha stands as far beyond the lord as the lord stands beyond the bhava. Suppose a bhava is in Cancer and the Moon, its lord, in Virgo. From Cancer to Virgo is 3, and 3 onward from Virgo is Scorpio, the 5th from Cancer, so no exception applies.

The arudha pada of the nth bhava is written An: A9 for the 9th. The 1st and the 12th have names instead: AL, the arudha lagna, and UL, the upapada lagna. A bhava arudha does not show the bhava's matter itself but what people go by when they judge it.

The book collects the traditional names for each one. Most join a word for the bhava's matter to *pada* or *arudha*, as with dhana (wealth) in dhana pada or dhanarudha for A2. Other such words are vikrama (valour) for A3, sukha (happiness) for A4, mantra (sacred formula) for A5, roga (illness) for A6, kashta (hardship) for A8, bhagya (fortune) for A9, karma (action) for A10 and labha (gain) for A11. hora keeps to the labels, plus the names arudha lagna, upapada lagna and darapada.

### Arudha lagna {#arudha-lagna}

The arudha lagna (AL) is the bhava arudha of the 1st bhava: how the world perceives one, and one's status. It is also a [reference point](/en/kb/bhava-significations#arudha-lagna): bhavas counted from it show matters as they stand in the material world and in other people's eyes.

- The 11th and 12th from AL show money coming in and money going out.
- [Kroora grahas](/en/kb/graha-characteristics#saumya-graha) (natural malefics) in the 3rd or 6th from AL give the name of someone who stands up to rivals and hits back, a name that tends to go with worldly success. Saumya grahas (natural benefics) there mark someone who is mild in public and avoids a fight.
- The 10th from AL in the dasamsa (D-10), the chart of career, shows one's standing at work, where the 10th from the lagna there shows how one really conducts oneself.

### Upapada lagna {#upapada-lagna}

The upapada lagna (UL) is the bhava arudha of the 12th bhava. It shows one's marriage and spouse, and is a reference point too. A graha in it describes the spouse and the marriage by that graha's own nature, at its best or its worst according to how the graha fares in the chart.

Who the spouse is and how long the marriage lasts are separate questions, and the second is read away from UL itself. Three bhavas counted from it bear on the marriage's survival: the 8th on its span, the 2nd and the 7th on its ending. They are the places to look for Mars, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu, whose presence there puts the marriage under strain, sometimes to the point of breaking it. So a difficult spouse and a lasting marriage can sit in one chart, as can a good spouse and a short one.

### Darapada {#darapada}

The darapada (A7) is the bhava arudha of the 7th bhava. The 7th shows one's relationships themselves; the darapada shows the people one actually associates with, since the company one keeps is what others judge those relationships by.

## Graha arudhas {#graha-arudha}

A graha arudha is the arudha pada of a graha, found with the same counting:

1. Take the rasi the graha stands in.
2. Take the rasi the graha owns. Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn own two, and the [stronger rasi](#stronger-rasi) is used. The Sun owns Leo, the Moon Cancer, Rahu Aquarius and Ketu Scorpio.
3. Count from the graha's rasi to the rasi it owns, in zodiac order, with the starting rasi as 1.
4. Count the same number onward from the rasi it owns.
5. If you land on the graha's own rasi or the 7th from it, go on to the 10th from where you landed.

A bhava arudha shows how the world sees a matter of one's life. Each graha stands for someone or something that comes into one's life, and its graha arudha shows how that looks from the inside, which can miss the facts as widely as the world's view of oneself misses them. That makes three ways of reading a graha together:

- **The graha counted from the lagna** shows what really happens.
- **The graha counted from AL** shows how the world sees it, and how things stand materially.
- **The graha's arudha pada counted from the lagna** shows how one feels about it.

The three often disagree, and none cancels the others. An adviser who reads only one will answer a question the person did not ask.

Rahu and Ketu show a pattern of their own. Where Ketu is in the 7th from Rahu, as in the rasi chart, their two graha arudhas fall in the same rasi; in a varga chakra where they stand together, in opposite rasis.

## The strength rules

Chapter 9 relies on rules the book teaches later, and so do hora's arudha padas: the strength comparisons of chapter 15, §15.5, which use the [rasi drishti](/en/kb/aspects-and-argalas#rasi-drishti) (sign aspect) of chapter 10, §10.3. Chapter 15 comes later in this learning path; only what arudha padas need is summarised here. In both comparisons the rules are tried in order and the first that separates the two decides. Only the nine grahas count as grahas here, never the lagna or a special lagna.

### Stronger lord {#stronger-lord}

Mars and Ketu both own Scorpio, and Saturn and Rahu both own Aquarius. For an arudha pada one of the two acts as lord: the stronger lord (§15.5.1). If exactly one of them is in the rasi in question, the other one acts. Otherwise the stronger is the one that:

1. has more grahas with it;
2. has more of Jupiter, Mercury and its dispositor (the lord of the rasi it occupies) with it or aspecting it by rasi drishti, where both lords of Scorpio or Aquarius count and Mercury counts again if he is the dispositor;
3. is exalted, where the other is not;
4. stands in the stronger kind of rasi, dual over fixed over movable;
5. is further advanced in its rasi, with Rahu's and Ketu's advancement measured back from the rasi's end.

This is why a lord here may differ from the chart page's *Rasi lord* column. That column always names Mars for Scorpio and Saturn for Aquarius, and [paaka lagna](/en/kb/bhava-significations#paaka-lagna) takes the same lord. In the arudha padas, Ketu or Rahu acts wherever it wins. If every rule ties, a case the book does not settle, hora takes Mars or Saturn.

### Stronger rasi {#stronger-rasi}

For a graha that owns two rasis, its graha arudha is counted from the stronger rasi (§15.5.2). The stronger of the two is the one that:

1. holds more grahas;
2. has more of Jupiter, Mercury and its lord in it or aspecting it by rasi drishti;
3. holds an exalted graha, where the other does not;
4. has its lord in an even rasi if it is itself odd, or in an odd rasi if it is itself even;
5. is of the stronger kind, dual over fixed over movable;
6. has the more advanced lord, with Rahu's and Ketu's advancement measured back from the rasi's end.

Scorpio and Aquarius count both their lords in rule 2 and their stronger lord in rules 4 and 6. If every rule ties, a case the book does not settle, hora takes the first of the two counted from Aries.

### Rasi drishti {#rasi-drishti}

Rasi drishti (§10.3) is an aspect between whole rasis, which a graha shares with the rasi it stands in; [Aspects and argalas](/en/kb/aspects-and-argalas#rasi-drishti) gives its rules.

## A worked example

Take hora's chart for 1 January 2000 at 12:00 UT, at Greenwich, with bhavas counted from the arudha lagna ([see the chart](/en/chart?date=2000-01-01&time=12%3A00&calendar=gregorian&utc=stated&offset=%2B00%3A00&latitude=51.48&longitude=0&altitude=&ayanamsa=lahiri&node=mean&sunrise=upper-limb-apparent&reference=arudha-lagna&style=south)). The lagna is in Aries, Jupiter and Saturn too; Rahu in Cancer, the Moon in Libra, Venus in Scorpio, the Sun and Mercury in Sagittarius, Ketu in Capricorn and Mars in Aquarius.

1. **The arudha lagna.** The lord of Aries is Mars, in Aquarius. From Aries to Aquarius is 11, and 11 onward from Aquarius is Sagittarius, the 9th from Aries, so no exception applies. AL is in Sagittarius, with the Sun and Mercury.
2. **The exception, A2.** The 2nd bhava is Taurus, whose lord Venus is in Scorpio. From Taurus to Scorpio is 7, and 7 onward from Scorpio is Taurus again, the bhava's own rasi. The 10th from Taurus is Aquarius, so A2 is in Aquarius.
3. **A stronger lord, A8.** The 8th bhava is Scorpio. Neither Mars nor Ketu is in it and each stands alone, so rule 1 ties. On rule 2, Mars in Aquarius is aspected by Jupiter and by both lords of Aquarius, Saturn from Aries and Rahu from Cancer, which makes three; Ketu in Capricorn is reached by none of Jupiter, Mercury or Saturn. Mars acts. From Scorpio to Aquarius is 4, and 4 onward from Aquarius is Taurus, the 7th from Scorpio. The 10th from Taurus is Aquarius, so A8 joins A2 there. With Ketu as lord it would have been Pisces.
4. **Reading from AL.** The Bhava column of the grahas table now counts from Sagittarius. Mars, a kroora graha, is in the 3rd, and the 6th, Taurus, is empty: this person comes across as bold. The Moon is in the 11th and Venus in the 12th, the bhavas of money coming in and going out.
5. **The upapada lagna.** The 12th bhava is Pisces, whose lord Jupiter is in Aries. From Pisces to Aries is 2, and 2 onward from Aries is Taurus, which holds no graha. Counted from Taurus, the 2nd, Gemini, is empty, the 7th, Scorpio, holds Venus, and the 8th, Sagittarius, holds the Sun and Mercury. None of Mars, Saturn, Rahu or Ketu is in those three.
6. **Three readings of Mars.** Aries holds Jupiter and Saturn and Scorpio only Venus, so Aries is Mars's stronger rasi. From Aquarius to Aries is 3, and 3 onward from Aries is Gemini, the graha arudha of Mars. Mars is in the 11th from the lagna, the bhava of gains; in the 3rd from AL, seen as bold; and his graha arudha is in the 3rd from the lagna, so one feels his matters as one's own effort and courage. For that last reading choose the lagna as the reference point ([see the chart](/en/chart?date=2000-01-01&time=12%3A00&calendar=gregorian&utc=stated&offset=%2B00%3A00&latitude=51.48&longitude=0&altitude=&ayanamsa=lahiri&node=mean&sunrise=upper-limb-apparent&reference=lagna&style=south)): counted from AL, the table shows Gemini as the 7th.
7. **A10 in the dasamsa.** Everything is found afresh in D-10 ([see the chart](/en/chart?date=2000-01-01&time=12%3A00&calendar=gregorian&utc=stated&offset=%2B00%3A00&latitude=51.48&longitude=0&altitude=&ayanamsa=lahiri&node=mean&sunrise=upper-limb-apparent&reference=lagna&style=south&varga=d10)). The lagna there is also in Aries, so the 10th bhava is Capricorn, whose lord Saturn is in Virgo. From Capricorn to Virgo is 9, and 9 onward from Virgo is Taurus. The Sun, one of the two kings among the grahas, stands in it: a workplace marked by rank and authority would suit it.

## In the chart

The chart page has two tables, *Arudha padas* for the bhava arudhas, AL to UL, and *Graha arudhas* for the nine grahas. Each gives the rasi and the bhava in the rasi chart and in the varga chakra shown, with bhavas counted from the chosen reference point. The chakras also carry the bhava arudhas as labels in italics, in every drawing style; graha arudhas appear only in their table. The *Count bhavas from* field on the home page offers *Arudha lagna* and *Upapada lagna*; in a varga chakra, each counts from that chart's own.
