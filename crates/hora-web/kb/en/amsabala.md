# Amsabala

A [graha](/en/kb/graha) (planet) in its uchcha (exaltation), moolatrikona or own rasi is strong in that chart; the [dignity](/en/kb/dignity) page explains the three. Since every [varga chakra](/en/kb/vargas) (divisional chart) is a chart of its own, a graha can hold such a rasi in some varga chakras and not in others. Amsabala, "strength by divisions", counts the varga chakras of a varga group (a fixed set of varga chakras) in which it does, and gives that count a name, the graha's amsa. The more such varga chakras, the stronger the graha.

## How the count works

For each varga chakra of the group, take the rasi the graha occupies in it and ask whether that rasi is the graha's uchcha, its moolatrikona or one of its own rasis. The count is the number of varga chakras where the answer is yes. The whole rasi counts: where the [dignity](/en/kb/dignity) page shares a rasi out by degree between moolatrikona and own rasi or uchcha, amsabala does not, since a varga chakra gives no degrees.

A count of two or more is named by an amsa of that group; a count of one or none has no name. Each group has its own list of names, below.

hora counts Rahu and Ketu too, by the uchcha, moolatrikona and own rasis the book gives them.

## Shadvarga {#shadvarga}

Shadvarga, "six vargas", is the rasi chart (D-1), hora (D-2), drekkana (D-3), navamsa (D-9), dwadasamsa (D-12) and trimsamsa (D-30). From a count of two up to all six, the amsas are kimsukaamsa, vyanjanaamsa, chaamaraamsa, chatraamsa and kundalaamsa.

## Sapta varga {#sapta-varga}

Sapta varga, "seven vargas", is shadvarga with the saptamsa (D-7) added. Its amsas are shadvarga's five, with mukutaamsa added for all seven.

## Dasa varga {#dasa-varga}

Dasa varga, "ten vargas", is sapta varga with the dasamsa (D-10), shodasamsa (D-16) and shashtyamsa (D-60) added. From a count of two up to all ten, the amsas are paarijaataamsa, uttamaamsa, gopuraamsa, simhaasanaamsa, paaraavataamsa, devalokaamsa, brahmalokamsa, airaavataamsa and sreedhaamaamsa.

Of the four, the book gives this group the most weight, since some yogas (special combinations) depend on its amsas.

## Shodasa varga {#shodasa-varga}

Shodasa varga, "sixteen vargas", is dasa varga with the chaturthamsa (D-4), vimsamsa (D-20), chaturvimsamsa (D-24), nakshatramsa (D-27), khavedamsa (D-40) and akshavedamsa (D-45) added: every varga chakra of the twenty but D-5, D-6, D-8 and D-11. From a count of two up to all sixteen, the amsas are bhedakaamsa, kusumaamsa, nagapurushaamsa, kandukaamsa, keralaamsa, kalpavrikshaamsa, chandanavanaamsa, poornachandraamsa, uchchaisravaamsa, dhanvantaryamsa, sooryakaantaamsa, vidrumaamsa, indraasanaamsa, golokaamsa and sree vallabhaamsa.

## A worked example

Take hora's chart for 1 January 2000 at 06:00 UT, at Greenwich ([see the chart](/en/chart?date=2000-01-01&time=06%3A00&calendar=gregorian&utc=stated&offset=%2B00%3A00&latitude=51.48&longitude=0&altitude=&ayanamsa=lahiri&node=mean&sunrise=upper-limb-apparent&reference=lagna&style=south)). Its Sun is at 16°15′59″ Sagittarius. The Sun's uchcha is Aries, and Leo is both his moolatrikona and his own rasi, so the count looks for Aries and Leo.

1. The Sun's rasi in the sixteen varga chakras of shodasa varga, from the chart page's Vargas table: D-1 Sagittarius, D-2 Cancer, D-3 Aries, D-4 Gemini, D-7 Pisces, D-9 Leo, D-10 Taurus, D-12 Gemini, D-16 Leo, D-20 Gemini, D-24 Virgo, D-27 Gemini, D-30 Sagittarius, D-40 Capricorn, D-45 Sagittarius, D-60 Leo.
2. Aries or Leo turns up four times: in D-3, D-9, D-16 and D-60.
3. Shadvarga holds two of them, D-3 and D-9: kimsukaamsa.
4. Sapta varga adds D-7, where the Sun is in Pisces, so its count stays two: kimsukaamsa.
5. Dasa varga holds all four: gopuraamsa.
6. Shodasa varga also holds all four, and none of the six varga chakras it adds brings another: nagapurushaamsa.

The same chart's Moon, whose uchcha and moolatrikona are Taurus and whose own rasi is Cancer, is in one of them in only one varga chakra of the sixteen, the hora (D-2), where she is in Cancer. That is a count of one in every group, and no amsa.

## In the chart

The chart page gives amsabala in a table of its own, under the Vargas table: a row for each of the nine grahas and a column for each group, holding the count and, from two up, the amsa. The lagna has no uchcha, moolatrikona or own rasi, and so no amsabala.
