# Varga chakra

A varga chakra (divisional chart) is a [chart](/en/kb/chart) built from a finer cut of the zodiac than the rasi chart (rasi chakra). Each [rasi](/en/kb/rasi) (sign) is split into equal parts, every part is given a rasi of its own, and each point goes into the rasi of the part it falls in. hora does not calculate varga chakras yet; the book teaches how in chapter 6.

## Parts of a rasi

A rasi spans 30°. Split into n equal parts, each part spans 30° divided by n: into 2 parts, each spans 15°; into 9 parts, each spans 3°20′.

Each part is then assigned to a rasi, which need not be the rasi it was cut from. Which part goes to which rasi is a rule of its own for each varga chakra; chapter 6 gives those rules. To draw the varga chakra, find for each [graha](/en/kb/graha) (planet) and for the lagna (ascendant) the part of its rasi that holds it, and write it into the rasi assigned to that part.

The book names each varga chakra D-n, after the n parts of its division. Cut each rasi into a single part, that is, not at all, and every part keeps its own rasi: the rasi chart is the plainest case of the rule.

## A chart of its own

A graha can be in one rasi in the rasi chart and in another in a varga chakra, and so can the lagna. A varga chakra is therefore read as a chart in its own right: its [bhavas](/en/kb/bhava) (houses) are counted afresh from where the lagna falls in it, and the grahas are judged by where they stand in it.

Each varga chakra is read for one area of life, and only for that area; the book lists which area each one shows in chapter 6.

The more parts a rasi is cut into, the smaller each part is, and the less a point has to move to land in another part. A finer varga chakra therefore asks for a more exact birth time.

## The four pillars

[Jyotish](/en/kb/jyotish) (Vedic astrology) rests on four ideas, each with its own page here:

1. [Graha](/en/kb/graha): the seven planets, with Rahu and Ketu.
2. [Rasi](/en/kb/rasi): the twelve signs of the zodiac they move through.
3. [Bhava](/en/kb/bhava): the twelve houses, the rasis counted from a reference point.
4. Varga chakra: the divisional charts, this page.
