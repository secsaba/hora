# Varga chakra

A varga chakra (divisional chart) is a [chart](/en/kb/chart) built from a finer cut of the zodiac than the rasi chart (rasi chakra). Each [rasi](/en/kb/rasi) (sign) is split into parts, equal in all varga chakras but one, every part is given a rasi of its own, and each point goes into the rasi of the part it falls in. The book teaches twenty of them in chapter 6, and hora calculates all twenty; [the twenty varga chakras](/en/kb/vargas) gives their rules.

## Parts of a rasi

A rasi spans 30°. Split into n equal parts, each part spans 30° divided by n: into 3 parts, each spans 10°; into 9 parts, each spans 3°20′.

Each part is then assigned to a rasi, which need not be the rasi it was cut from. Which part goes to which rasi is a rule of its own for each varga chakra; chapter 6 gives those rules, and names each varga chakra D-n after its number of parts. Where a point lands in a varga chakra so depends on two things: how far into its rasi it stands, which picks the part, and the varga chakra's rule, which picks the rasi of that part. This holds for every point of a chart: each [graha](/en/kb/graha) (planet), the lagna (ascendant), the [upagrahas](/en/kb/upagraha) (sub-planets) and the [special lagnas](/en/kb/special-lagna) (special ascendants).

## A chart of its own

A graha can be in one rasi in the rasi chart and in another in a varga chakra, and so can the lagna. A varga chakra is therefore read as a chart in its own right: its [bhavas](/en/kb/bhava) (houses) are counted afresh from where the lagna falls in it, and the grahas are judged by where they stand in it.

Each varga chakra is read for one area of life; [the twenty varga chakras](/en/kb/vargas) says which area each one shows.

## The four pillars

Everything in [Jyotish](/en/kb/jyotish) (Vedic astrology) is built from four ideas, and this page completes them. The [grahas](/en/kb/graha) are what moves; the [rasis](/en/kb/rasi) are where on the zodiac each graha stands; the [bhavas](/en/kb/bhava) are where it stands as seen from a reference point of the chart; and the varga chakras look at all of that again, rasi by rasi, at a finer grain.

## In the chart

The chart page draws one varga chakra beside the rasi chart, the navamsa (D-9) unless another is picked, and lists every body's rasi in all twenty; [the twenty varga chakras](/en/kb/vargas) describes both.

Next come the [nakshatras](/en/kb/nakshatra): the zodiac cut into twenty-seven parts instead of twelve.
