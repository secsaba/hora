# Ravi and Chandra yogas

Ravi yogas (solar combinations) are formed around the Sun, Chandra yogas (lunar combinations) around the Moon (§11.2–11.3). Most of them look at the rasis on either side of the luminary. The general cautions that apply to every yoga are on the [yogas](/en/kb/yogas#caveats) page.

## Ravi yogas {#ravi}

Mercury and Venus never stray far from the Sun: usually they share his rasi or stand one rasi off. So the first three yogas below are very common in the rasi chart and much rarer in a [varga chakra](/en/kb/varga-chakra) (divisional chart). The book suggests reading them in the navamsa (D-9) and dasamsa (D-10) above all.

In each of them "a planet" is one of the seven, so Rahu and Ketu form none.

### Vesi yoga {#vesi}

**Rule:** a planet other than the Moon stands in the 2nd rasi from the Sun. With the Sun in Gemini, for instance, any planet but the Moon in Cancer gives it.

**Results:** a balanced outlook; truthful; tall; slow to act; happy and at ease even with little money.

### Vosi yoga {#vosi}

**Rule:** a planet other than the Moon stands in the 12th rasi from the Sun. With the Sun in Aries, any planet but the Moon in Pisces gives it.

**Results:** skilful; generous; famous; learned; strong.

### Ubhayachara yoga {#ubhayachara}

**Rule:** planets other than the Moon stand in both the 2nd and the 12th from the Sun. With the Sun in Cancer, Mars in Leo and Venus in Gemini give it.

**Results:** every comfort; a king, or one of a king's standing.

### Budha-Aaditya yoga {#budha-aaditya}

Budha is Mercury, Aaditya the Sun. The yoga is also called Nipuna yoga, nipuna meaning an expert.

**Rule:** the Sun and Mercury are in the same rasi.

**Results:** intelligent; skilled in every kind of work; well known; respected; happy.

A planet too close to the Sun is asta (combust), and a yoga formed by a combust planet does less good. This yoga is strongest in varga chakras such as the D-10; in the rasi chart it can still give its results if Mercury is not combust. With the Sun and Mercury together in Leo in the D-10, for example, the native has a strong Budha-Aaditya yoga in career, felt through life and most of all in the dasas of the Sun, Mercury and Leo. hora lists the yoga whenever the two share a rasi and leaves its power to you.

## Chandra yogas {#chandra}

### General guidelines {#guidelines}

Before its lunar yogas, the book gives three guidelines (§11.3). They describe tendencies rather than yogas, so hora does not compute them.

1. **The Moon from the Sun.** With the Moon in a [kendra](/en/kb/bhava-categories#kendra) (quadrant) from the Sun, one may have little wealth, intelligence and skill; in a panaphara (succedent), an average share; in an apoklima (cadent), a great deal.
2. **The Moon's navamsa and its aspects.** The Moon in its own navamsa, or in that of an adhimitra (good friend), is good. Then Jupiter's aspect on the Moon brings wealth and comforts to one born by day, and Venus's aspect does the same for one born by night. The other way round, Jupiter's aspect in a night birth and Venus's in a day birth harm wealth and comforts.
3. **Benefics in upachayas from the Moon.** With every natural benefic in an [upachaya](/en/kb/bhava-categories#upachaya) (house of growth: the 3rd, 6th, 10th or 11th) from the Moon, wealth is great; with two of them there, it is middling; with only one, it is small.

### Sunaphaa yoga {#sunaphaa}

**Rule:** planets other than the Sun stand in the 2nd from the Moon. With the Moon in Gemini, any planet but the Sun in Cancer gives it.

**Results:** a king or one of a king's standing; intelligent; wealthy; famous; wealth earned by oneself.

### Anaphaa yoga {#anaphaa}

**Rule:** planets other than the Sun stand in the 12th from the Moon. With the Moon in Aries, any planet but the Sun in Pisces gives it.

**Results:** a king; good-looking; a body free of disease; of good character; a great reputation; surrounded by comforts.

### Duradhara yoga {#duradhara}

**Rule:** planets other than the Sun stand in both the 2nd and the 12th from the Moon. With the Moon in Cancer, Mars in Leo and Venus in Gemini give it.

**Results:** many pleasures; generous; wealth and vehicles; good servants.

### Kemadruma yoga {#kemadruma}

**Rule:** a bad yoga with two conditions together. No planet other than the Sun is in the 1st, 2nd or 12th from the Moon, and no planet other than the Moon is in a kendra from the lagna. With a Taurus lagna and the Moon in Virgo, for example, the yoga needs no planet but the Sun in Leo, Virgo or Libra and no planet at all in Taurus, Leo, Scorpio or Aquarius. Rahu and Ketu, not being among the seven planets, do not count in either check.

**Results:** unlucky; without intelligence or learning; beset by poverty and trouble. It destroys the results of the chart's other good yogas, the Chandra yogas above all. The native has to labour hard and succeeds only by great effort.

### Chandra-Mangala yoga {#chandra-mangala}

**Rule:** the Moon and Mars are in the same rasi.

**Results:** worldly-wise; successful in material things; may make money by unscrupulous means; may treat the mother, or other women, badly.

### Adhi yoga {#adhi}

**Rule:** natural benefics occupy the 6th, 7th and 8th from the Moon. The book's example, with the Moon in Taurus, Mercury and Jupiter in Virgo and Venus in Leo, puts them in the 5th and 4th instead; the rule wins over its example. hora counts the yoga when two or more natural benefics stand in those three places, in any arrangement, not counting the Moon herself. A Mercury who is kroora (malefic) by his company is not one of them.

**Results:** a king, a minister or a commander of an army, as strong as the planets that form it allow.
