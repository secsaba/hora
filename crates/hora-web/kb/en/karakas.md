# Karakas

A karaka (significator) is a [graha](/en/kb/graha) (planet) that stands for some matter in a life and brings about what happens to it. The word means "the one who makes it happen". Any one matter, a child say, has more than one karaka, and each answers a different kind of question about it. This page covers the three kinds of karaka, the eight chara karakas hora shows in its chart table, and the atma karaka as a reference point.

## Three kinds, never mixed

The three kinds of karaka do three separate jobs, and the book is firm that a question asked of one kind must not be answered with another. Each kind belongs to one of the three gods of the Hindu trinity, and the god's role explains the job.

### Naisargika karakas {#naisargika}

Naisargika karakas (natural significators) belong to Brahma, the creator, and between them cover everything in creation that touches a person: people, but also things, places and ideas. They are the same in every chart and all nine grahas are among them. They are used for the general run of results.

### Chara karakas {#chara}

Chara karakas (variable significators) belong to Vishnu, who keeps the world going, and show the people who sustain one, help one achieve and take part in one's spiritual growth: advisers, parents, children, a partner. They change from chart to chart, and only the seven planets and Rahu can be one. Ketu cannot: he stands for moksha (liberation), not for anyone one lives through. Chara karakas are the ones to use for raja yogas (combinations for power) and for the path of the soul, and they show the karma (accumulated deeds) one brings from life to life.

### Sthira karakas {#sthira}

Sthira karakas (fixed significators) belong to Shiva, who ends things, and are used to time the death of close relatives. Only the seven planets are sthira karakas, since only they have bodies, Rahu and Ketu being shadows.

## Finding the chara karakas

The order of the chara karakas is decided by how far each graha has advanced through the rasi (sign) it is in:

1. Take the Sun, the Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn and Rahu; leave Ketu out.
2. For each of the seven planets, note its degrees within its rasi. Rahu moves backwards through the zodiac, so his advancement is counted from the far end of his rasi: 30° minus his degrees.
3. Rank the eight from the most advanced to the least. Compare degrees first; if they are equal, the minutes; if those are equal too, the seconds.

The most advanced is the atma karaka, and the rest follow in order. Each karaka stands for a kind of person in one's life:

### Atma karaka {#atma-karaka}

1st, AK: oneself, the soul behind the person, and so the inner nature.

### Amatya karaka {#amatya-karaka}

2nd, AmK: those who advise one, counsellors and ministers.

### Bhratri karaka {#bhratri-karaka}

3rd, BK: brothers and sisters.

### Matri karaka {#matri-karaka}

4th, MK: the mother.

### Pitri karaka {#pitri-karaka}

5th, PiK: the father, and also a boss.

### Putra karaka {#putra-karaka}

6th, PK: children, and also those who follow one or work under one.

### Jnaati karaka {#jnaati-karaka}

7th, GK: rivals and enemies. The word itself means a cousin on the father's side.

### Dara karaka {#dara-karaka}

8th, DK: the spouse.

Two grahas at exactly the same advancement, to the second, hold one karakatwa (signification) together, and the next one in the order has no chara karaka; the book then takes the sthira karaka of that relative instead. hora shows the shared karakatwa and leaves the next one empty, since sthira karakas are not computed yet. A tie to the second is rare.

Other schools use only seven chara karakas, leaving out Rahu and the pitri karaka. hora uses the book's eight and offers no choice between the two.

## The atma karaka as a reference point {#karakamsa}

The atma karaka stands for the soul, which shapes the inner self more than the body does, so it serves as a [reference point](/en/kb/bhava-significations#karakamsa) for counting [bhavas](/en/kb/bhava) (houses), above all in the navamsa (D-9), the varga chakra of the inner self. The rasi the atma karaka occupies in the navamsa is the karakamsa, and the navamsa is read from it as its 1st bhava (chapter 7, §7.3.6). The 12th bhava from the karakamsa shows the soul's release: Ketu there helps it towards moksha, and worshipping the deity of the strongest graha there is a remedy that leads the same way.

The karakamsa is the navamsa rasi of the atma karaka, not the navamsa's lagna, and the two need not be the same rasi.

## Sthira karakas, relative by relative

The sthira karakas are the same in every chart:

| Relative | Sthira karaka |
|---|---|
| Father | The Sun or Venus, whichever is stronger |
| Mother | The Moon or Mars, whichever is stronger |
| Younger brothers and sisters, and their spouses | Mars |
| Uncles and aunts on the mother's side | Mercury |
| Husband, sons, and the father's side of the family, grandparents, uncles and aunts | Jupiter |
| Wife, parents-in-law, and the mother's parents | Venus |
| Elder brothers and sisters | Saturn |

Choosing the stronger of two grahas needs the strength rules of chapter 15, so hora names neither sthira karaka of the father or the mother yet.

A sthira karaka stands for the relative's own body: to time the husband's death in a woman's chart, the book looks at Jupiter himself. A chara karaka works the same way: the dara karaka is the spouse, and one does not count the 7th bhava from it. A naisargika karaka works differently, and a sthira karaka is never used in its place: marriage, in a man's chart or a woman's, is read from the 7th bhava from Venus, not from Jupiter.

## Naisargika karakas, matter by matter

A naisargika karaka does not stand for the matter itself. The matter is read from a bhava counted from the karaka: the mother is the 4th bhava from the Moon, children the 5th from Jupiter, the spouse and married life the 7th from Venus. The pairs, grouped by the area of life they belong to:

- **The person:** the self, body, health and soul, the 1st from the Sun; the mind, the 1st from the Moon.
- **Family:** the mother and peace of mind, the 4th from the Moon; the father, the 9th from the Sun; the spouse and married happiness, the 7th from Venus; children, the 5th from Jupiter; younger brothers and sisters, the 3rd from Mars; elder ones, the 11th from Jupiter; the household and its wealth, the 2nd from Jupiter.
- **Others around one:** friends, the 11th from the Moon; a teacher, the 9th from Jupiter; a boss, the 9th from the Sun; servants, the 6th from Saturn; followers, the 5th from Saturn; enemies, the 6th from Mars.
- **Mind and learning:** speech, the 2nd from Mercury; learning, the 4th from Mercury, and traditional learning the 4th from Jupiter; memory, scholarship and students, the 5th from Mercury; intelligence, the 5th from Jupiter; logic and speculation, the 5th from Mars.
- **Work and standing:** work, achievements and honours, the 10th from Mercury; career, the 10th from the Sun; fame and power, the 5th from the Sun.
- **Property, gain and loss:** land and houses, the 4th from Mars; vehicles, the 4th from Venus; gains, the 11th from Jupiter; money owed to one, the 11th from Mercury; loans, the 6th from Mars; losses and time in hospital, the 12th from Saturn.
- **Courage and trouble:** courage, the 3rd from Mars; illness and accidents, the 6th from Mars, and accidents also the 6th from Rahu; longevity and hardship, the 8th from Saturn.
- **Faith and the hidden:** religion and fortune, the 9th from Jupiter; pilgrimage and travel abroad, the 9th from Rahu and from Ketu; the occult, the 8th from Rahu and from Ketu; moksha, the 12th from Ketu; the pleasures of the bed, the 12th from Venus.

So memory, for instance, is the 5th bhava counted from Mercury.

## Which karaka for which question

Take children. Each kind of karaka answers its own question about them:

- Whether children come, and the everyday events of their lives: the 5th bhava from Jupiter, the naisargika karaka.
- Joy, success or grief through children, the kind that touches the soul: the putra karaka, the chara karaka.
- When a child's life ends: Jupiter himself, the sthira karaka of sons.

## A worked example

Take hora's chart for 1 January 2000 at 12:00 UT, at Greenwich, with the grahas table in chara karaka order ([see the chart](/en/chart?date=2000-01-01&time=12%3A00&calendar=gregorian&utc=stated&offset=%2B00%3A00&latitude=51.48&longitude=0&altitude=&ayanamsa=lahiri&node=mean&sunrise=upper-limb-apparent&reference=lagna&style=south&sort=karaka)).

1. The advancements, from the Degree column: the Moon 19°28′, Saturn 16°32′, the Sun 16°31′, Mercury 8°02′, Venus 7°43′, Mars 4°07′, Jupiter 1°24′. Rahu is at 11°11′ of Cancer, so his advancement is 30° − 11°11′ = 18°49′. Ketu is left out.
2. The Moon is the most advanced, and so the atma karaka. Rahu, counted from the end of his rasi, comes second, the amatya karaka.
3. Saturn and the Sun are both at 16°, so the minutes decide: Saturn's 32′ beat the Sun's 31′, making Saturn the bhratri karaka and the Sun the matri karaka.
4. The rest follow: Mercury the pitri karaka, Venus the putra karaka, Mars the jnaati karaka and Jupiter, the least advanced, the dara karaka.
5. Children, three ways: the 5th bhava from Jupiter, in Aries, is Leo, which holds no graha; the putra karaka is Venus, in Scorpio; and Jupiter is the sthira karaka of sons.
6. In the navamsa the Moon is in Pisces, so Pisces is the karakamsa ([see the chart](/en/chart?date=2000-01-01&time=12%3A00&calendar=gregorian&utc=stated&offset=%2B00%3A00&latitude=51.48&longitude=0&altitude=&ayanamsa=lahiri&node=mean&sunrise=upper-limb-apparent&reference=atma-karaka&style=north&varga=d9)), the top diamond of the North Indian drawing. The 12th bhava from it is Aquarius, which holds no graha in the navamsa; Ketu is in Aries, the 2nd.

## In the chart

The chart page's grahas table has a *Chara karaka* column, each karaka linking to its section here; its heading sorts the table into chara karaka order, from the atma karaka down to the dara karaka, and the *Graha* heading sorts it back. The *Count bhavas from* field on the home page offers the atma karaka as a reference point; in the navamsa, counting from it counts from the karakamsa. Chara karakas come from the rasi chart's degrees and are the same in every varga chakra. Naisargika and sthira karakas are the same in every chart, so the chart page does not list them.
