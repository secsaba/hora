# Special lagna

The lagna (ascendant), the point of the zodiac rising in the east at birth, stands for the self. A special lagna (special ascendant) is another point of the zodiac that is read the same way: the [bhavas](/en/kb/bhava) (houses) can be counted from it, and the [grahas](/en/kb/graha) (planets) judged by where they stand from it. Each special lagna shows the self from one side of life. The book teaches four of them in chapter 5: three that move at fixed speeds from sunrise, and one that follows from the lagna and the Moon.

## The three that count from sunrise

The hora, ghati and bhaava lagnas all start at the same place: the Sun's longitude at the sunrise that opened the birth's [Vedic day](/en/kb/vaara-and-hora). At that moment all three stand on the Sun. From there each moves forward through the zodiac at its own constant speed, much faster than any graha, so its position at birth depends only on how long after that sunrise the birth came. A birth after midnight but before the Sun is up still belongs to the Vedic day that began the morning before, so the count starts at that earlier sunrise.

The speeds are measured by the clock, not in horas of the Vedic day: an hour here is 60 minutes, and a ghati is a flat 24 minutes, a sixtieth of 24 hours, whatever the length of that particular Vedic day. Past 360° the count simply goes round again.

Where a birth has no Vedic day, as in polar night or under the midnight sun, there is no sunrise to count from, and none of the three can be found.

The book prints a hora lagna and a ghati lagna in nearly every chart it shows, and those printed values fit a sunrise up to about four minutes later than the one its own text recommends. hora follows the text, counting from the moment the upper edge of the Sun's disc appears, with refraction, unless the form chooses another sunrise; so for the book's charts its hora lagna can differ from the printed one by up to about 2°, and its ghati lagna by up to about 5°.

## Hora lagna {#hora-lagna}

The hora lagna (HL) moves one rasi (sign) every hour: 30° an hour, or half a degree a minute. It shows the self as seen through wealth: money, possessions and prosperity.

## Ghati lagna {#ghati-lagna}

The ghati lagna (GL) moves one rasi every ghati of 24 minutes: 1°15′ a minute, two and a half times as fast as the hora lagna. It shows the self as seen through power and fame: authority, standing and recognition.

Its speed makes it sensitive to the birth time. A birth time off by one minute moves the ghati lagna by 1°15′, and one rounded to the nearest five minutes leaves it uncertain by about 3° either way; in the [varga chakras](/en/kb/varga-chakra) (divisional charts), where a rasi is cut into small parts, that can be the difference between one part and another. So the ghati lagna is only as good as the birth time behind it. The book also shows how to work backwards, adjusting a birth time until the ghati lagna lands where known events say it should; hora does not do that, and takes the birth time as given.

## Sree lagna {#sree-lagna}

The sree lagna (SL) is found from the lagna and the Moon, with no sunrise involved. Take the [nakshatra](/en/kb/nakshatra) (constellation) the Moon is in, and see what fraction of it the Moon has already crossed. Take the same fraction of the whole circle of 360°, and add it to the lagna's longitude, going round again past 360°. If the Moon has just entered its nakshatra, the sree lagna is close to the lagna; halfway through, it is opposite; near the end, it has come almost all the way round.

"Sree" means wealth and good fortune, and the sree lagna shows prosperity. The book puts it to work in chapter 20, where Sudasa, a [dasa](/en/kb/dasa) (period) system of rasis, starts its periods from the rasi holding the sree lagna.

## Bhaava lagna {#bhaava-lagna}

The bhaava lagna (BL) moves one rasi every two hours: 15° an hour, half the speed of the hora lagna. The book defines it only to complete the set and does not use it anywhere, so hora lists it but offers it neither as a reference point nor in the drawing.

## A worked example

Take hora's chart for 1 January 2000 at 06:00 UT, at Greenwich ([see the chart](/en/chart?date=2000-01-01&time=06%3A00&calendar=gregorian&utc=stated&offset=%2B00%3A00&latitude=51.48&longitude=0&altitude=&ayanamsa=lahiri&node=mean&sunrise=upper-limb-apparent&reference=lagna&style=south)). Its lagna is at 17°14′32″ Scorpio and its Moon at 16°27′38″ Libra.

1. At 06:00 the Sun had not yet risen over Greenwich, so the birth belongs to the Vedic day that began on Friday, 31 December, at a sunrise the chart page shows as 08:05:49, the first whole second after it. hora counts from the exact moment, 21 hours 54 minutes and 11.6 seconds before the birth: 21.9032 hours.
2. At that sunrise the Sun stood at 15°20′11″ Sagittarius, a longitude of 255°20′11″.
3. Hora lagna: 21.9032 hours at 30° an hour is 657°05′49″. Added to the Sun's 255°20′11″ it makes 912°26′00″; taking away two full turns leaves 192°26′00″, which is 12°26′00″ Libra.
4. Ghati lagna: 1314.19 minutes at 1°15′ a minute is 1642°44′33″. Added to the Sun's longitude it makes 1898°04′44″; five full turns less, 98°04′44″, which is 8°04′44″ Cancer.
5. Bhaava lagna: 21.9032 hours at 15° an hour is 328°32′55″. With the Sun's longitude that is 583°53′05″, one turn less 223°53′05″: 13°53′05″ Scorpio, in the lagna's own rasi.
6. Sree lagna: the Moon's nakshatra is Swaati, which runs from 6°40′ to 20°00′ Libra. The Moon has crossed 9°47′38″ of its 13°20′, a fraction of about 0.73455. The same fraction of 360° is about 264°26′. Added to the lagna's longitude, 227°14′32″, it makes about 491°41′, and one turn less 131°41′: about 11°41′ Leo. The chart page, working without rounding, gives 11°40′42″.

## In the chart

The chart page lists the special lagnas in a table of their own, between the grahas and the [upagrahas](/en/kb/upagraha) (sub-planets). Each row gives the rasi, the bhava counted from the chosen reference point, the position, the nakshatra and pada (quarter), the nakshatra's Vimsottari lord and the rasi's lord. The drawing marks the hora and ghati lagnas, as HL and GL, in their rasis. The hora, ghati and sree lagnas can each be chosen as the reference point that the bhavas are counted from. A chart without a Vedic day lists only the sree lagna. hora places the special lagnas in the rasi chart only; in the varga chakras they wait for chapter 6.
