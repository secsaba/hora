# Dhana and Daridra yogas

Dhana means wealth and daridra poverty. Dhana yogas give abundant riches (§11.9); Daridra yogas bring poverty (§11.10). Lords here are lords in a yoga, as on the [yogas](/en/kb/yogas#readings) page, and "conjoined or aspected" means in the same rasi or aspecting with [graha drishti](/en/kb/aspects-and-argalas#graha-drishti). The general cautions that apply to every yoga are on the [yogas](/en/kb/yogas#caveats) page.

## Dhana yogas {#dhana}

### Basic Dhana yoga {#basic-dhana}

The book opens with a principle (§11.9). Money can come from the 5th and 9th lords and from whatever planets join them, during their dasas. The 11th, which rules material gain, should be strong, and the 2nd counts as well. Of all this, hora computes only the one rule with a clear test.

**Rule:** the Moon, Mercury, Jupiter or Venus is exalted in the 2nd.

**Results:** very rich.

### Parasara's combinations for each lagna {#by-lagna}

Parasara gives two combinations for each lagna. The first makes one very affluent, the second very rich, and in all of them the strength of the planets decides how much. In the second, the lagna's planet must be conjoined or aspected by each of the planets named. A row says which combination held, or both.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (1): Aries lagna {#dhana-1}

**Rule:** the Sun in the 5th, and Saturn, the Moon and Jupiter in the 11th. Or Mars in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mercury, Venus and Saturn.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (2): Taurus lagna {#dhana-2}

**Rule:** Mercury in the 5th, and the Moon, Mars and Jupiter in the 11th. Or Venus in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mercury and Saturn.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (3): Gemini lagna {#dhana-3}

**Rule:** Venus in the 5th and Mars in the 11th. Or Mercury in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Jupiter and Saturn.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (4): Cancer lagna {#dhana-4}

**Rule:** Mars in the 5th and Venus in the 11th. Or the Moon in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mercury and Jupiter.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (5): Leo lagna {#dhana-5}

**Rule:** Jupiter in the 5th and Mercury in the 11th. Or the Sun in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mars and Jupiter.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (6): Virgo lagna {#dhana-6}

**Rule:** Saturn in the 5th, and the Sun and the Moon in the 11th. Or Mercury in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Jupiter and Saturn.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (7): Libra lagna {#dhana-7}

**Rule:** Saturn in the 5th, and the Sun and the Moon in the 11th. Or Venus in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mercury and Saturn.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (8): Scorpio lagna {#dhana-8}

**Rule:** Jupiter in the 5th and Mercury in the 11th. Or Mars in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mercury, Venus and Saturn.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (9): Sagittarius lagna {#dhana-9}

**Rule:** Mars in the 5th and Venus in the 11th. Or Jupiter in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mars and Mercury.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (10): Capricorn lagna {#dhana-10}

**Rule:** Venus in the 5th and Mars in the 11th. Or Saturn in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mars and Jupiter.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (11): Aquarius lagna {#dhana-11}

**Rule:** Mercury in the 5th, and the Moon, Mars and Jupiter in the 11th. Or Saturn in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mars and Jupiter.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

### Dhana yoga §11.9 (12): Pisces lagna {#dhana-12}

**Rule:** the Moon in the 5th and in the 11th. Or Jupiter in the lagna, conjoined or aspected by Mars and Mercury.

**Results:** very affluent by the first; very rich by the second.

The first combination is a misprint as it stands: the Moon cannot be in both houses. The other lagnas suggest a second planet was meant for the 11th, but the book does not say which, so hora computes only the second combination for Pisces.

## Daridra yogas {#daridra}

Several of these rules turn on marakas (killers). The 2nd and 7th are the maraka houses and their lords are marakas; so are the malefics that occupy those houses or associate with their lords. hora reads the last as malefics conjoining or aspecting, with graha drishti, the 2nd or 7th or their lords, in the chart being read (§14.2). In a rule asking a maraka to touch a graha, that maraka must be another graha than the one touched.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (1) {#daridra-1}

**Rule:** the lagna lord is in the 12th and the 12th lord in the lagna, and each of them is conjoined or aspected by a maraka, the same one or not.

**Results:** poverty.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (2) {#daridra-2}

**Rule:** the lagna lord is in the 6th and the 6th lord in the lagna, and each of them is conjoined or aspected by a maraka, the same one or not.

**Results:** poverty.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (3) {#daridra-3}

**Rule:** the lagna or the Moon is with Ketu, the lagna lord is in the 8th, and a maraka conjoins or aspects the lagna lord.

**Results:** poverty.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (4) {#daridra-4}

**Rule:** the lagna lord is with a malefic in a [dusthana](/en/kb/bhava-categories#dusthana) (the 6th, 8th or 12th), and the 2nd lord is debilitated or in an enemy's rasi.

**Results:** poverty, even for one born a prince.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (5) {#daridra-5}

**Rule:** the 5th lord is in the 6th and the 9th lord in the 12th, each aspected by a maraka.

**Results:** poverty.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (6) {#daridra-6}

**Rule:** malefics occupy the lagna without the 9th and 10th lords there, and are conjoined or aspected by marakas.

**Results:** poverty.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (7) {#daridra-7}

**Rule:** the dispositors of the 6th, 8th and 12th lords (the lords of the rasis those three occupy) are each in the 6th, 8th or 12th, conjoined or aspected by malefics.

**Results:** poverty.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (8) {#daridra-8}

**Rule:** the lord of the rasi the Moon occupies in the navamsa (D-9) is with a maraka, or in a maraka house, the 2nd or 7th. Rasi chart only.

**Results:** poverty.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (9) {#daridra-9}

**Rule:** the lagna lords of the rasi chart and of the navamsa are conjoined or aspected by marakas. Rasi chart only.

**Results:** poverty.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (10) {#daridra-10}

**Rule:** benefics are in malefic houses and malefics in benefic houses.

**Results:** poverty.

The chapter does not say which houses are benefic or malefic, and the "benefic houses" of ch. 12 belong to ashtakavarga, different for each planet. So hora does not compute this one.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (11) {#daridra-11}

**Rule:** a planet conjoined by the 6th, 8th or 12th lord, and neither conjoined nor aspected by a lord of a trikona (the 1st, 5th or 9th). hora lists each such planet with the dusthana lords joining it.

**Results:** loss of wealth in that planet's dasas.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (12) {#daridra-12}

**Rule:** Mars and Saturn are in the 2nd, and Mercury does not aspect them. Mercury's only aspect is on his 7th, so this means Mercury is not in the 8th.

**Results:** poverty. The exception: if Mercury does aspect Mars and Saturn in the 2nd, they generate great wealth instead. hora lists the yoga only when the aspect is missing.

### Daridra yoga §11.10 (13) {#daridra-13}

**Rule:** the Sun in the 2nd is aspected by Saturn.

**Results:** poverty. The exception: without Saturn's aspect, the Sun in the 2nd gives wealth. hora lists the yoga only when the aspect is there.

### What the Daridra yogas teach {#principles}

The book sums the rules up: wealth suffers from the dusthanas and their lords, and when these afflict the lagna, its lord and the trikona lords, poverty may follow, a maraka joining or aspecting making it certain. A trikona lord joining or aspecting is the rescue. Whatever stands in the 2nd, and how strong its lord is, also counts.
