# Marakas and Rudra

Chapter 14 gathers the tools the book later uses to judge the length of a life and to time its end with dasas. This page covers the rasis and grahas that kill or bring suffering: the marakas (§14.2), Rudra, the Trishoola rasis and Maheswara (§14.3). The methods that estimate how long a life is, and the book's caution about using any of this, are on the [longevity](/en/kb/longevity) page.

## Houses of life and death {#houses}

Two bhavas carry life. The 3rd shows vitality, the strength of one's existence, and the 8th shows how long that existence lasts. The 12th from any bhava shows the loss of that bhava's matters, so the 12th from the 3rd, the 2nd, and the 12th from the 8th, the 7th, are the houses of death. A long life wants the 3rd and 8th and their lords strong, and the 2nd and 7th and their lords weak.

Death is an event of the body, so the rasi chart matters most. The book also looks at two [varga chakras](/en/kb/vargas): the [D-11](/en/kb/vargas#d-11), which shows the forces of destruction, and the [D-30](/en/kb/vargas#d-30), which shows one's wrongs and what they bring on, death among them.

## Marakas {#marakas}

Maraka means killer. Every chart has maraka rasis and maraka grahas, and when a dasa is used to time death, the book looks for them in it. Transits can be read for them too.

### Maraka sthanas {#maraka-sthanas}

The rasis of the 2nd and 7th bhavas are the maraka sthanas (killer stations). In a dasa of rasis that can show death, their dasas can bring it.

### Maraka grahas {#maraka-grahas}

The lords of the 2nd and 7th are maraka grahas (killer grahas). So is a malefic that conjoins, or aspects with [graha drishti](/en/kb/aspects-and-argalas#graha-drishti), the 2nd or the 7th or one of their lords. In a dasa of grahas that can show death, their dasas can bring it.

A made-up case: the lagna is in Cancer, so the 2nd is Leo and the 7th Capricorn. The Sun and Saturn, their lords, are marakas. Mars in Taurus is one as well: his 4th-house aspect falls on Leo, the 2nd. Rahu in Capricorn, a malefic in the 7th, is another.

The book says a malefic must conjoin or aspect "powerfully". It gives no measure for that, so hora lists every malefic that conjoins or aspects at all, and names each reason: which house it owns, occupies or aspects, and which lord it joins or aspects. Where Mercury's company leaves his [nature](/en/kb/graha-characteristics#saumya-graha) undecided, hora lists him only as a lord.

### Exercise 23 {#exercise-23}

The book's exercise is chart 8, with the lagna in Scorpio (p. 186). The 2nd is Sagittarius and the 7th Taurus, so Jupiter and Venus own them, and Rahu stands in Taurus: these three are the book's main marakas. The book adds that Mercury, the 8th lord, may also be considered, as he joins Jupiter and Venus. That is a reading beyond §14.2's rule, which Mercury does not meet here, so hora leaves him out; weigh him yourself. hora also lists the Sun and Mars, both in Scorpio, as their 7th-house aspect falls on Taurus, which the rule asks for though the book's answer does not name them.

## Rudra {#rudra}

Indian myth knows eleven Rudras, forms of Shiva that bring suffering and death. Each chart has eleven rasis standing for them, each for suffering in one area of life. The one that matters most brings suffering to the body itself, and the graha that plays that part is called simply Rudra: it stands for the native's suffering and destruction.

Two grahas are the candidates: the lord of the 8th from the lagna and the lord of the 8th from the 7th. The stronger of them is Rudra.

### The 8th by Table 32 {#table-32}

For Rudra the 8th is not counted in the usual way but read from the book's Table 32. Its footnote 50 explains how it is built: odd rasis count forward and even rasis backward, and the rasis of Brahma and Vishnu move one way and those of Shiva another, a motion the book teaches with Narayana dasa. Until that chapter, hora uses the table as printed. Its 8th from each rasi is:

- Aries: Scorpio; Taurus: Gemini; Gemini: Capricorn; Cancer: Sagittarius;
- Leo: Cancer; Virgo: Aquarius; Libra: Taurus; Scorpio: Sagittarius;
- Sagittarius: Cancer; Capricorn: Gemini; Aquarius: Capricorn; Pisces: Leo.

For a Scorpio or Aquarius 8th, which have two lords, hora takes the [stronger lord](/en/kb/arudha-padas#stronger-lord).

### Saturn in the lagna or the 7th {#saturn}

The book sets Table 32 aside once, in a worked chart of a later chapter: with Saturn in the lagna, it counts both 8ths, from the lagna and from the 7th, in the normal way (ch. 23, p. 276). hora does the same whenever Saturn is in the lagna or the 7th. Chapter 18 gives further cases where the motion changes; no worked example of chapter 14's tools uses them, and hora does not yet.

### Which is stronger {#strength}

§14.3 gives its own list for this choice, not chapter 15's. The rules are tried in order, and the first that separates the two decides. The stronger is the one that:

1. has more grahas with it;
2. is [exalted](/en/kb/dignity#uchcha) or in its [own rasi](/en/kb/dignity#own-rasi);
3. has exalted grahas with it;
4. is aspected by more grahas with [rasi drishti](/en/kb/aspects-and-argalas#rasi-drishti);
5. is further advanced in its rasi.

If all five tie, a case the book does not settle, hora takes the lord of the 8th from the lagna.

### The afflicted weaker {#afflicted}

The weaker candidate can be Rudra too, when it is afflicted: [debilitated](/en/kb/dignity#neecha) or in the rasi of a [natural](/en/kb/graha-relationships#natural) enemy, and conjoined or aspected by Mars, Saturn, Rahu or Ketu. The book's wording can be read either way, as the weaker taking Rudra's place or as standing beside him. hora lists both, and draws the Trishoola rasis from the afflicted weaker, because that is the only reading that gives the book's answers on both charts it works:

- In chart 8, Jupiter, the weaker, is in Libra, the rasi of his enemy Venus, under Rahu's rasi drishti from Taurus. The book still calls Mercury Rudra (p. 186), and both stand in Libra, so the Trishoola rasis are the same either way.
- In chart 61, the weaker, Rahu, is debilitated and under Ketu's rasi drishti from Gemini, and the book draws the Trishoola from him as the better candidate (p. 276).

The aspect here is rasi drishti. Rahu and Ketu have no graha drishti in hora, and with graha drishti chart 61's Rahu would not be afflicted at all (ADR 0017).

### Chart 8's Rudra {#chart-8}

By Table 32 the 8th from Scorpio is Sagittarius, Jupiter's, and the 8th from Taurus, the 7th, is Gemini, Mercury's. Both are in Libra, so they share their company and their aspects, and neither is exalted or at home. Mercury, at 28° of Libra, is further on than Jupiter at 21°, so Mercury is Rudra.

## Trishoola rasis {#trishoola}

The three trines from Rudra's rasi in the rasi chart, that rasi and the 5th and 9th from it, are the Trishoola rasis, the three spikes of Shiva's trident (trishoola). They are rasis that can bring death: in Shoola dasa, one of them, chosen by whether the life is short, middle or long, does so in its dasa. That use is on the [longevity](/en/kb/longevity#shoola-dasa) page. In chart 8, Rudra is in Libra, so the Trishoola rasis are Gemini, Libra and Aquarius.

## Maheswara {#maheswara}

Maheswara is the supreme form of Shiva, who frees the soul. The [atma karaka](/en/kb/karakas#atma-karaka) (AK) stands for the soul, and the lord of the 8th from it, counted in the normal way, is Maheswara: the channels through which the soul strives for liberation. Three exceptions change him:

1. If that lord is in his own or exaltation rasi, the stronger of the lords of the 8th and the 12th from him is Maheswara instead.
2. If Rahu or Ketu is with the AK or in the 8th from it, take the lord of the 6th from the AK in place of the 8th: the 8th counted backward.
3. If Rahu turns out to be Maheswara, Mercury takes his place; if Ketu, Jupiter does.

A made-up case: the AK is Jupiter in Virgo. The 8th from Virgo is Aries, so Mars would be Maheswara; but say Mars is exalted in Capricorn. From Capricorn, the 8th is Leo and the 12th Sagittarius, so the stronger of the Sun and Jupiter is Maheswara. Had Ketu stood with Jupiter in Virgo, the 6th from Virgo, Aquarius, would have been counted instead.

hora applies the exceptions in the order (2), (1), (3). When both (2) and (1) apply, which the book never shows, it counts the second step the way the first was counted: the 6th and the 2nd from the lord, the 8th and 12th counted backward. A node that is itself the AK does not count as joining it. The stronger in (1) is decided by chapter 15's rules for comparing two grahas.

In chart 8 the AK is Mercury in Libra. The 8th from Libra, Taurus, holds Rahu, so by (2) the 6th, Pisces, is counted, and its lord Jupiter, in Libra and not at home, is Maheswara, as the book finds.

## What the book leaves open {#open}

- **The weaker as Rudra:** whether he replaces the stronger or joins him. hora lists both, as above.
- **Scorpio's and Aquarius's lord:** hora takes the stronger lord. On chart 40 the book takes Ketu as the 8th lord from Aries, where the stronger lord is Mars (pp. 276–277). Rudra, Venus, is the same either way; the longevity result is not (see the [longevity](/en/kb/longevity#differences) page).
- **When Table 32 gives way:** only Saturn's case is shown in a worked chart. In Example 56 the book calls Jupiter Rudra, the 8th lord from Leo counted in the normal way (p. 222); by Table 32 the candidates are the Moon and Saturn, and hora gives Saturn.
- **Marakas' strength:** "powerfully" has no measure, so every malefic that qualifies is listed.

## On the chart page {#chart-page}
