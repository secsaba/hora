# Graha relationships

[Grahas](/en/kb/graha) (planets) behave like people with likes and dislikes. A graha in another's [rasi](/en/kb/rasi) (sign) is a guest of that rasi's lord (the graha that owns it), and how it fares there depends partly on what it thinks of its host. The relationship is built in three layers: a natural one that never changes, a tatkaala (temporary) one that comes from a single chart, and a compound one that combines the two.

## Natural relationship {#natural}

By nature, each of the seven planets sees each of the others as a mitra (friend), a sama (neutral) or a satru (enemy), the same in every chart. Rahu and Ketu are left out of this scheme.

The rule starts from the planet's [moolatrikona](/en/kb/dignity#moolatrikona) rasi. Counting that rasi as the 1st, the lords of the 2nd, 4th, 5th, 8th, 9th and 12th rasis are on its side, and so is the lord of its [uchcha](/en/kb/dignity#uchcha) rasi. The lords of the remaining rasis, the 1st, 3rd, 6th, 7th, 10th and 11th, are against it. Since most planets own two rasis, a planet can come out on both sides at once; then it is neutral.

Take the Sun, whose moolatrikona is Leo. Virgo, the 2nd from Leo, is Mercury's, but so is Gemini, the 11th: Mercury lands on both sides and is neutral. Mars owns the 4th (Scorpio) and the 9th (Aries), which is also the Sun's uchcha rasi: a friend. Jupiter owns the 5th and 8th, the Moon the 12th: friends. Venus owns the 3rd and 10th, Saturn the 6th and 7th: enemies.

Natural relationships need not be returned. The Moon counts no planet as her enemy, while Mercury counts her as his.

## Tatkaala relationship {#tatkaala}

The tatkaala relationship belongs to one chart. Counting from the rasi a graha occupies, the grahas in the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 10th, 11th and 12th rasis are its tatkaala mitras (temporary friends): its near neighbours on either side. Those in the same rasi and in the 5th to 9th, across the zodiac, are its tatkaala satrus (temporary enemies).

Unlike the natural relationship, the tatkaala one always runs both ways: the 2nd from one rasi is the 12th from the other, the 3rd the 11th and the 4th the 10th.

## Compound relationship {#compound}

The compound relationship weighs the two together. A tatkaala friendship lifts the natural relationship one step, a tatkaala enmity drops it one step, giving five values from adhimitra (good friend) to adhisatru (bad enemy):

When a reading speaks of a graha's friend or enemy in a chart, it means the compound relationship. A graha in a rasi whose lord is its mitra or adhimitra is in a friendly house; one whose lord is its satru or adhisatru is in an inimical house. A sama lord makes it neither.

A graha in its own rasi is its own host, so it has no relationship to weigh there. Rahu and Ketu have no natural relationships, and so no compound ones.

## A worked example

Take hora's chart for 1 January 2000 at 06:00 UT, at Greenwich ([see the chart](/en/chart?date=2000-01-01&time=06%3A00&calendar=gregorian&utc=stated&offset=%2B00%3A00&latitude=51.48&longitude=0&altitude=&ayanamsa=lahiri&node=mean&sunrise=upper-limb-apparent&reference=lagna&style=south)). Saturn and Jupiter are in Aries, whose lord Mars is in Aquarius; Mercury is in Sagittarius, whose lord Jupiter is in Aries.

1. Saturn in Aries. By nature Mars is Saturn's satru. Aquarius is the 11th rasi from Aries, so Mars is Saturn's tatkaala mitra. An enemy lifted one step is sama: Saturn's house is neither friendly nor inimical. Aries is also Saturn's [neecha](/en/kb/dignity#neecha) rasi; his dignity and his relationship with his host are read side by side.
2. Jupiter in Aries. By nature Mars is Jupiter's mitra, and counted from Aries, as for Saturn, he is a tatkaala mitra too: adhimitra. Jupiter is in a friendly house.
3. Mercury in Sagittarius. By nature Jupiter is sama to Mercury. Aries is the 5th rasi from Sagittarius, so Jupiter is Mercury's tatkaala satru: satru. Mercury is in an inimical house.

## In the chart

The *Rasi lord* column of the chart page's table names the lord of each graha's rasi, then, in parentheses, the graha's compound relationship with it, linked to this page. It shows only the lord for a graha in its own rasi, for Rahu and Ketu, and for the lagna (ascendant).
