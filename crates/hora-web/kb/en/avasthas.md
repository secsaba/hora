# Avasthas

An avastha is a state a [graha](/en/kb/graha) (planet) is in. A capable person still works badly when tired, distracted or unhappy, and a graha's state likewise colours how well it gives its results. Chapter 15 describes four families of avasthas (§15.4): by age, by alertness, by attitude and mood, and by activity. The last, the Sayanaadi avasthas, matter most. The other ways of measuring a graha are on the [strength](/en/kb/strength) page.

hora reads every family from the rasi chart, with bhavas counted from the lagna, whatever reference point the form names (ADR 0018). The age states need degrees, which a [varga chakra](/en/kb/varga-chakra) does not have, and the other families follow so that one table holds them all.

## By age {#age}

A child and a very old person both have limits, and so does a graha at the start or the end of a rasi (§15.4.1). Its degree in the rasi gives one of five states, in 6° steps counted forward in an odd rasi (Aries, Gemini, Leo…) and backward in an even one:

- **Saisava** (child): the first 6° of an odd rasi, the last 6° of an even one. A quarter of its results.
- **Kumaara** (adolescent): 6°–12° odd, 18°–24° even. Half its results.
- **Yuva** (youth): 12°–18° in both. Its full results.
- **Vriddha** (old): 18°–24° odd, 6°–12° even. Some results.
- **Mrita** (dead): the last 6° of an odd rasi, the first 6° of an even one. No results.

So a graha at 10° of Taurus, an even rasi, is Vriddha, and at 10° of Gemini, an odd one, Kumaara. hora gives Rahu and Ketu an age by their degrees in the rasi, counted the same way.

The book warns against taking these too far: child prodigies and wise old heads exist, and a graha can do well whatever its age. Age is one factor among many.

## By alertness {#alertness}

Three states, read from the rasi the graha is in (§15.4.2):

- **Jaagrita** (awake): in its [uchcha](/en/kb/dignity#uchcha) (exaltation) or an [own rasi](/en/kb/dignity#own-rasi). Full results.
- **Swapna** (dreaming): in the rasi of a friend or a neutral. Medium results.
- **Sushupta** (asleep): in its [neecha](/en/kb/dignity#neecha) (debilitation) or an enemy's rasi. Next to no results.

A friend, neutral or enemy is the graha's [compound relationship](/en/kb/graha-relationships#compound) with the rasi's lord among the seven planets: mitra or adhimitra a friend, sama a neutral, satru or adhisatru an enemy, as everywhere else in hora. Rahu and Ketu have no relationships, so a node is Jaagrita in its own or exaltation rasi, Sushupta in its debilitation, and otherwise in no alertness state.

## By attitude and mood {#mood}

The book lists nine states and then six more (§15.4.3). They read the rasi and the graha's company, and more than one can hold at once; hora lists every one that holds, in the book's order.

The nine:

1. **Deepta** (bright): in its uchcha.
2. **Swastha** (at ease, content): in an own rasi.
3. **Mudita** (delighted): in a good friend's rasi, adhimitra.
4. **Saanta** (peaceful): in a friend's rasi, mitra.
5. **Deena** (sad, downcast): in a neutral's rasi, sama.
6. **Duhkhita** (distressed, wretched): in an enemy's rasi, satru or adhisatru.
7. **Vikala** (crippled, confused): joined by a malefic.
8. **Khala** (scheming, mischievous): in a malefic's rasi.
9. **Kopita** (angry): joined closely by the Sun.

The six:

1. **Lajjita** (ashamed): in the 5th bhava, joined by the Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu or Ketu.
2. **Garvita** (proud): in its uchcha or [moolatrikona](/en/kb/dignity#moolatrikona) rasi.
3. **Kshudhita** (hungry): in an enemy's rasi, or joined or aspected by an enemy, or joined by Saturn.
4. **Trishita** (thirsty): in a watery rasi (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces), aspected by an enemy and by no benefic.
5. **Mudita** (delighted) again: in a friend's rasi, joined or aspected by friends, and joined by Jupiter.
6. **Kshobhita** (shaken, agitated): joined by the Sun and aspected by a malefic or an enemy.

The book reads a few of them for the bhava they stand in. Grahas in Kshudhita or Kshobhita spoil what their bhava signifies. A Lajjita graha in the 5th can bring losses through children. A Kshobhita graha in the 7th can mean losing the spouse.

### How hora reads them {#mood-readings}

The rules are loosely worded, and hora reads them as follows (ADR 0018):

- **Friend, neutral, enemy** are the compound relationship, as for alertness. The same relationship decides which grahas joining or aspecting a graha are its friends or enemies.
- **Malefic and benefic** are the natural ones, [saumya and kroora](/en/kb/graha-characteristics#saumya-graha), as in the yogas.
- **Aspects** are [rasi drishti](/en/kb/aspects-and-argalas#rasi-drishti), as the strength rules of §15.5 use them.
- **Kopita**'s "closely" is left to you: hora has no orbs for how near the Sun must be, so it marks every graha in the Sun's rasi, and the chart page says that the closeness is to judge.
- **Lajjita** is a graha joined by any one of the five, not all five at once.
- **Khala** holds in a malefic's own rasi too, as the book words it.
- **Deepta** and **Garvita** count the whole rasi of uchcha or moolatrikona, not the degrees within it.
- **Mudita** holds by either of its two definitions.
- **No graha joins himself**, so the Sun is never Kopita, Kshobhita or Lajjita through his own presence.
- **Rahu and Ketu** take only the states read from a rasi or from company: Deepta, Swastha, Vikala, Khala, Kopita, Lajjita and Garvita, and Trishita read as a watery rasi with no benefic's aspect, since nothing can be a node's enemy. The states that need a relationship do not apply to them.

## By activity: the Sayanaadi avasthas {#sayanaadi}

The twelve states of activity are the most important avasthas (§15.4.4). They are named after the first, Sayana, and run:

1. **Sayana**: lying down, resting.
2. **Upavesana**: sitting down.
3. **Netrapaani**: eyes and hands.
4. **Prakaasana**: shining.
5. **Gamana**: going, on the move.
6. **Aagamana**: coming back.
7. **Sabhaa**: at an assembly.
8. **Aagama**: coming, acquiring.
9. **Bhojana**: eating.
10. **Nriyalipsaa**: longing to dance.
11. **Kautuka**: eager.
12. **Nidraa**: sleeping.

### Finding the state {#formula}

Six numbers go in:

- **C**: the [nakshatra](/en/kb/nakshatra) the graha is in, 1 for Aswini to 27 for Revati.
- **P**: the graha, 1 for the Sun, 2 the Moon, 3 Mars, 4 Mercury, 5 Jupiter, 6 Venus, 7 Saturn. The book gives no number for the nodes; hora takes 8 for Rahu and 9 for Ketu, continuing the same order.
- **A**: which ninth of its rasi the graha is in, each 3°20′ long, 1 to 9, counted from the rasi's start. This is the part of the rasi, not the rasi the graha holds in the navamsa. hora counts the nodes from the rasi's start too.
- **M**: the Moon's nakshatra, numbered as for C.
- **G**: the ghati running at birth, counting 24-minute ghatis from the sunrise that opened the [Vedic day](/en/kb/vaara-and-hora). A birth 5 hours 10 minutes after sunrise, 310 minutes, is 12.9 ghatis on, in the 13th: G is 13. A night birth before sunrise counts from the sunrise of the morning before.
- **L**: the lagna's rasi, 1 for Aries to 12 for Pisces.

Multiply C, P and A, add M, G and L, and divide by 12. The remainder is the state's number, a remainder of 0 being Nidraa, the 12th.

Where a birth has no Vedic day, as in a polar night, there is no G, and the chart page says that it has no Sayanaadi avastha.

### Its strength {#strength}

How fully the graha gives the results of its state is one of three degrees:

1. Square the state's number and add the number of the name sound (below). Divide by 12 and keep the remainder.
2. Add the graha's own number: 5 for the Sun and Jupiter, 2 for the Moon and Mars, 3 for Mercury, Venus and Saturn, 4 for Rahu and Ketu.
3. Divide by 3. A remainder of 1 is **drishti**, medium results; 2 is **cheshta**, full results; 0 is **vicheshta**, very little. This drishti is not an aspect.

### Name sound {#name-sound}

The strength needs the first sound of the native's given name. The book sorts the sounds into five groups, and the birth form offers them as it prints them, with "Not given" as the sixth choice. Choose by how the name begins when said aloud, not by how it is spelled: the groups are sounds of Sanskrit, and the letters of a name's spelling may stand for other sounds. hora does not map any spelling to a group, the Hungarian cs, sz or gy no more than any other; the choice is yours.

hora asks only for the group, never for the name, and keeps it in the chart's address like the other birth data. Without it the chart page shows each graha's state but not its strength, and says why.

### A worked example {#example}

Take hora's chart for 1 January 2000 at 12:00 UT at Greenwich, with a name in the first group ([see the chart](/en/chart?date=2000-01-01&time=12%3A00&calendar=gregorian&utc=stated&offset=%2B00%3A00&latitude=51.48&longitude=0&altitude=&ayanamsa=lahiri&node=mean&sunrise=upper-limb-apparent&reference=lagna&style=south&sound=1&topic=strength)), and find Jupiter's state.

1. Jupiter is at 1°24′ of Aries, in Aswini: C = 1. He is Jupiter: P = 5. 1°24′ is in the first 3°20′: A = 1.
2. The Moon is in Swaati, the 15th nakshatra: M = 15.
3. The Sun rose at 08:05:47, so 3 hours 54 minutes had passed by noon, 9.76 ghatis: the 10th was running, G = 10.
4. The lagna is in Aries: L = 1.
5. 1 × 5 × 1 + 15 + 10 + 1 = 31, and 31 divided by 12 leaves 7: Sabhaa.
6. The strength: 7 × 7 + 1 = 50, which leaves 2 after dividing by 12. Jupiter's number 5 makes 7, and 7 divided by 3 leaves 1: drishti. Jupiter gives Sabhaa's results to a medium degree.

## What each Sayanaadi avastha gives {#results}

The classics ascribe these results to each graha in each state; the book lists them in §15.4.4. They are given to the degree the strength says. A condition in italics narrows a result to the case it names, a bhava being counted from the lagna.

### Sayana {#sayana}

- **Sun:** digestive trouble and other illness, heavy legs, excess bile, piles, heart trouble.
- **Moon:** respected but sluggish, lustful, squanders money.
- **Mars:** troubles, wounds, ulcers, itching.
- **Mercury:** given to pleasures, dissolute, wicked, always hungry; *in the lagna,* lame, with eyes like a bee's.
- **Jupiter:** strong, but with a weak voice, a tawny complexion and prominent cheeks; afraid of enemies.
- **Venus:** strong, bad teeth, quick-tempered, poor, dissolute.
- **Saturn:** hunger and thirst, illness in childhood, wealth later on.
- **Rahu:** many sorrows; *in Aries, Taurus, Gemini or Virgo,* wealth.
- **Ketu:** many illnesses; *in Aries, Taurus, Gemini or Virgo,* wealth.

### Upavesana {#upavesana}

- **Sun:** poor, quarrelsome, hard-hearted, loses money.
- **Moon:** illness, dull-witted, poor, merciless, does wrong, steals money.
- **Mars:** strong, sinful, a liar, eminent, wealthy.
- **Mercury:** *in the lagna,* of good character; *joined or aspected by malefics,* poor; *joined or aspected by benefics,* wealthy and happy.
- **Jupiter:** talkative, harassed by enemies and by kings, ulcers on the hands and feet.
- **Venus:** gems and gold, destroys enemies, honoured by kings, happy.
- **Saturn:** self-respecting, punished by kings, harassed by enemies, meets dangers, ulcers.
- **Rahu:** suffers from ulcers, close to royalty, respected, but short of money.
- **Ketu:** suffers from ulcers, trouble from enemies, snakes and thieves, illnesses of vaata (wind).

### Netrapaani {#netrapaani}

- **Sun:** always happy, wise, helps others, strong, wealthy, enjoys pleasures, a favourite of kings.
- **Moon:** serious illness, talks too much, wicked, always does bad deeds.
- **Mars:** *in the lagna,* penury; *elsewhere,* rules a town.
- **Mercury:** respected, but without wisdom, learning or well-wishers; *in the 5th,* no happiness from wife and children, many daughters, money from royal patronage.
- **Jupiter:** without health or wealth, lustful, fond of amusements, a tawny complexion, keeps company with people of another class.
- **Venus:** *in the lagna, the 7th or the 10th,* eye trouble and loss of wealth; *elsewhere,* a large house.
- **Saturn:** a charming wife, learned in the arts, royal favour, friends, intelligent, a good speaker.
- **Rahu:** eye trouble, loss of money, trouble from wicked men, snakes and thieves.
- **Ketu:** eye trouble, trouble from wicked men, snakes, thieves and rulers.

### Prakaasana {#prakaasana}

- **Sun:** generous, wealthy, eloquent in assemblies, strong, handsome.
- **Moon:** famous in the world, virtuous, royal patronage and royal insignia, wealth, luxuries, frequent visits to shrines, many ornaments.
- **Mars:** virtuous and honoured by kings; *in the 5th,* loss of children; *in the 5th with Rahu,* a severe fall.
- **Mercury:** generous, kind, does good deeds, learned in many subjects, wise, destroys bands of wicked men.
- **Jupiter:** virtuous, pure happiness and comfort, splendour, visits the holy places of Krishna; *exalted,* great fame across the world and great opulence.
- **Venus:** *exalted, or in its own or a friend's rasi,* bears itself like a mighty elephant, the equal of a king, fond of poetry, the arts and music.
- **Saturn:** virtuous, intelligent, opulent, fond of amusements, kind, a devotee of Shiva.
- **Rahu:** prospers abroad, virtuous, wealthy, a high position, good deeds, a chief at a king's court, charming.
- **Ketu:** wealthy, righteous, lives abroad, enthusiastic, sincere, serves the king.

### Gamana {#gamana}

- **Sun:** lives abroad, unhappy, lazy, unwise, poor, troubled by fears, angry.
- **Moon:** *waning,* sinful, cruel, eye trouble; *waxing,* troubled by fear.
- **Mars:** wanders, boils and itching, quarrels with women, many wounds and ulcers.
- **Mercury:** often at kings' courts, wealthy.
- **Jupiter:** brave, many friends, learned, wealthy.
- **Venus:** the mother does not live long, separated from one's own people, fear of enemies.
- **Saturn:** rich, good sons, a scholar at a king's court, seizes other people's land.
- **Rahu:** many children, learned, wealthy, generous, honoured by a king.
- **Ketu:** many children, learned, wealthy, generous, virtuous, a great man.

### Aagamana {#aagamana}

- **Sun:** affairs with other men's wives, deserted by people, fond of travel, a cheat, unclean.
- **Moon:** respected, illness of the feet, hidden sins, poor, dull-witted, sad.
- **Mars:** of good character, has gems and weapons, destroys enemies.
- **Mercury:** often at kings' courts, wealthy, the same as in Gamana.
- **Jupiter:** servants, fine women and wealth always grace the home.
- **Venus:** wealthy, visits holy shrines, enthusiastic, illness of the hands and feet.
- **Saturn:** separated from the family, foolish, wanders, miserable.
- **Rahu:** irritable, dull-witted, poor, wicked, miserly, lustful.
- **Ketu:** illness, loss of wealth, a tale-bearer, hurts others.

### Sabhaa {#sabhaa}

- **Sun:** helps others, has wealth, gems and land, virtuous, very strong, affectionate and kind.
- **Moon:** eminent, honoured by kings, sexual pleasures, of good character.
- **Mars:** a scholar at a king's court, wealthy, respected, generous; *exalted,* skilled in war, successful, upholds dharma; *in a trine,* unlearned; *in the 12th,* no happiness from wife and children.
- **Mercury:** *exalted,* great wealth and good deeds; and wealth, a king or a minister, devotion to Vishnu and Shiva, a saattwik nature, moksha.
- **Jupiter:** fine speech, the best gems and wealth, royal insignia, great learning.
- **Venus:** eminent at a king's court, virtuous, destroys enemies, wealthy and generous.
- **Saturn:** shows fine knowledge of law in assemblies, great wealth and gems, very brilliant.
- **Rahu:** learned, miserly, many virtues, wealthy, happy.
- **Ketu:** talks too much, proud, miserly, dissolute, skilled in evil arts.

### Aagama {#aagama}

- **Sun:** harassed by enemies, fickle, a cheat, thin, strays from good dharma and karma, wanton.
- **Moon:** *waxing,* talks too much, virtuous; *waning,* two wives, sickly, a cheat, wicked.
- **Mars:** bad character and deeds, ear trouble, timid, friends with evil-minded people.
- **Mercury:** earns by serving low people, two sons, and a daughter who brings a good name.
- **Jupiter:** many vehicles, comforts, honours, servants, happiness through children, knowledge, friends, learning, a noble path.
- **Venus:** no wealth, trouble from enemies, separation from children, illness, unhappiness through the spouse.
- **Saturn:** illness, unskilled, no royal patronage.
- **Rahu:** loss of money, fear of enemies and lawsuits, low spirits, separated from one's own people, thin, manipulative.
- **Ketu:** notorious, sinful, lawsuits with one's own people, illness, enemies.

### Bhojana {#bhojana}

- **Sun:** pain in the joints, loses wealth through the opposite sex, loss of strength, lies and spreads lies, headaches, a bad path.
- **Moon:** *waxing,* standing, honour, vehicles, a wife and daughters, servants; *waning,* these come hard.
- **Mars:** *strong,* sweet food; *weak,* mean deeds, dishonour.
- **Mercury:** loses money in lawsuits, the anger of kings, no happiness in marriage.
- **Jupiter:** excellent food, wealth, royal insignia.
- **Venus:** *debilitated,* wealthy, respected by scholars; *otherwise,* suffers hunger, illness and fear of enemies.
- **Saturn:** weak sight, delusion, enjoys tasty food.
- **Rahu:** no happiness from wife and children, without good food, timid.
- **Ketu:** hungry, sick, wanders, poor.

### Nriyalipsaa {#nriyalipsaa}

- **Sun:** learned, a scholar, good at discussing poetry, revered by kings.
- **Moon:** *waxing,* strong, knows poetry and music, a connoisseur of the arts; *waning,* sinful.
- **Mars:** wealth through royal connections, gold, diamonds and coral.
- **Mercury:** honour, vehicles, gems, valour, friends, children, learned in assemblies; *in a malefic's rasi,* dissolute, visits prostitutes.
- **Jupiter:** honoured by kings, wealthy, learned in dharma sastra and tantra, revered by the powerful and respected by the learned, a scholar faultless in grammar and etymology.
- **Venus:** skilled in literature, the arts and music, meritorious, opulent.
- **Saturn:** righteous, opulent, honoured by kings, brave and heroic in war.
- **Rahu:** serious illness, eye trouble, fear of enemies, loss of money.
- **Ketu:** illness, eye trouble, wicked, sinful.

### Kautuka {#kautuka}

- **Sun:** happy, learned, performs yajnas (rites), lives in a king's palace, good at discussing poetry, handsome.
- **Moon:** a king, wealthy, skilled in lovemaking.
- **Mars:** curious, friends and children; *exalted,* honoured by the king, virtuous.
- **Mercury:** *in the lagna,* skilled in music; *in the 7th or the 8th,* given to courtesans; *in the 9th,* good deeds and character, goes to heaven.
- **Jupiter:** curious, very kind, happy, honoured, sons, famous.
- **Venus:** opulent, learned, famous and respected in assemblies.
- **Saturn:** owns land, wealthy, happy, pleasures with charming women, learned in poetry and the arts.
- **Rahu:** wanders, pursues other men's wives, steals other men's wealth.
- **Ketu:** pursues courtesans, losses in work, sinful, wanders.

### Nidraa {#nidraa}

- **Sun:** drowsy, lives abroad, troubles for the wife.
- **Moon:** *with Jupiter,* eminent; *without Jupiter,* loses wealth through women, troubles.
- **Mars:** angry, dull-witted, poor, wicked, fallen from the virtuous path, sick.
- **Mercury:** restless sleep, trouble with the neck, miseries, lawsuits with one's own people, loses wealth.
- **Jupiter:** poor, dull-witted, foolish, no righteous deeds.
- **Venus:** serves others, criticises others, talks too much, wanders.
- **Saturn:** rich, charming, of good character, brave, destroys all enemies, skilled with women.
- **Rahu:** virtuous, a good wife and children, happy, proud, bold, wealthy.
- **Ketu:** virtuous, wealth and farm produce, passes the time in amusements.

### Parasara's special results {#special-results}

Parasara adds results that depend on the bhava, counted from the lagna, and on whether the graha is a benefic or a malefic:

- A benefic in Sayana does good to the bhava it is in.
- A malefic in Nidraa in the 7th, with no other malefic joining or aspecting it, is auspicious.
- A malefic in Bhojana destroys the bhava it is in.
- A malefic in Sayana or Nidraa in the 5th is auspicious.
- A malefic in Sayana or Nidraa in the 8th brings death through a king's anger.
- A malefic in Bhojana or Sayana in the 10th brings every kind of misery.
- A benefic, or a graha in its own or its exaltation rasi, in the 1st, 5th, 7th or 10th, in Prakaasana, Nriyalipsaa or Kautuka, gives a [Raaja yoga](/en/kb/raaja-yogas) (a combination for power). Parasara names the Moon in the 10th in Kautuka or Prakaasana in particular.

## A caution {#caution}

The book ends the section with a warning (§15.4.4). Avasthas are states of a graha and no more. Its age, alertness, mood and activity play a part in what it gives, but the bhavas it influences in the various varga chakras weigh more. Predicting from rules of thumb and lookup lists, such as the results above, is a temptation to resist.

## In the chart {#in-the-chart}

The chart page's *Strength* topic has an *Avasthas* table: each graha's age state and the share of results it gives, its alertness, every mood state that holds, its Sayanaadi avastha and, when the name sound is given, that avastha's strength. A "—" means no state of that family applies. The table shows the states only; what they give is on this page.
