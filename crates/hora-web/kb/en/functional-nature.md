# Functional nature

Every graha has a natural nature: it is a [saumya graha](/en/kb/graha-characteristics#saumya-graha) (natural benefic) or a kroora graha (natural malefic). On top of that, the bhavas it owns, counted from a lagna, give it a **functional nature**: it is a functional benefic, a functional neutral or a functional malefic for that lagna (§13.2). The two answer different questions. The natural nature is what a graha is like; the functional nature is whether it does good or harm to this chart. A kind person can still hurt you, and a harsh one can still help you: a natural benefic can be a functional malefic, and a natural malefic a functional benefic.

Rahu and Ketu have no functional nature: the book gives them none.

## The rules {#rules}

The book reads a graha's lordships from the lagna this way:

- The lord of a [trikona](/en/kb/bhava-categories#trikona) (trine: the 1st, 5th or 9th) is a functional benefic.
- The lord of the 3rd, 6th or 11th is a functional malefic.
- The lord of a [kendra](/en/kb/bhava-categories#kendra) (quadrant: the 4th, 7th or 10th) is a functional malefic if he is a natural benefic, and functionally neutral if he is a natural malefic. Owning a kendra takes something away from a graha's own nature: it spoils a benefic and calms a malefic.
- The lord of the 2nd, 8th or 12th is functionally neutral, the 8th being the worst of the three.

A graha that owns two rasis gets two readings, and the book asks for them to be combined with judgement. hora's reading of that, which the table mostly bears out: two readings that agree stand; a benefic with a neutral makes a benefic; a malefic with a neutral makes a malefic. A graha that owns one benefic and one malefic house is left open by the rules.

## Yogakaraka {#yogakaraka}

A graha that owns both a kendra and a trikona is the **yogakaraka** (excellent planet) of that lagna. The lagna itself counts as neither here, so a lagna lord who owns one other good house is not one. Only six lagnas have a yogakaraka, and each of them has just one: Saturn for Taurus and Libra, Mars for Cancer and Leo, and Venus for Capricorn and Aquarius. For the other six, no graha owns both.

## The book's table {#table}

The book lists each lagna's yogakaraka, functional benefics, neutrals and malefics in Table 30, and hora uses that table exactly as printed (ADR 0016). The *Functional nature* column of the chart page shows its verdict for every graha; this page does not repeat the table.

### The Moon in movable lagnas {#moon}

The table leaves the Moon out for Aries, Libra and Capricorn lagnas. For each of them she owns a kendra (Cancer is the 4th, 10th or 7th), so her verdict turns on her own nature, which changes with her phase. Waxing, in Sukla paksha, she is a natural benefic, and the kendra makes her a functional malefic. Waning, in Krishna paksha, she is a natural malefic, and the kendra leaves her neutral. hora decides by the [paksha](/en/kb/graha-characteristics#paksha) of the chart. Cancer is a movable rasi too, but there the Moon owns the lagna, a trikona, and the table makes her a benefic.

### Where the table departs from the rules {#departures}

Checked cell by cell against the rules above, the table does not always follow them. In five places it gives a different answer from the one the rules decide:

- for Taurus, the Sun, lord of the 4th only, is a benefic, though a natural malefic owning a kendra should be neutral;
- for Gemini, Saturn, lord of the 8th and 9th, is neutral, though a trikona with a neutral house should make him a benefic;
- for Libra, Mars, lord of the 2nd and 7th, is a malefic, though both his houses should make him neutral;
- for Sagittarius, Mercury, lord of the 7th and 10th, is neutral, though a natural benefic owning kendras should be a malefic;
- for Aquarius, Mercury, lord of the 5th and 8th, is neutral, though a trikona with a neutral house should make him a benefic.

In nine more, a graha owns one benefic and one malefic house, which the rules leave open, and the table settles each case: for example a lagna lord who also owns the 6th, as Venus does for Taurus (a malefic) and Mars for Scorpio (neutral).

### Where it agrees with Parasara {#parasara}

The table reads as Parasara's lists in the *Brihat Parasara Hora Sastra* (ch. 34), corrected by the rules where he plainly breaks one (ADR 0016). All five departures above are Parasara's own verdicts, and so are most of the open cases. Where Parasara breaks a rule, the table follows the rule instead: Venus for a Libra lagna is a benefic in the table, but neutral in Parasara; the table names Mars and Saturn as the yogakarakas of Leo and Libra, where Parasara names none. Parasara often uses *yogakaraka* for a pair of grahas whose union gives a yoga, not for the single owner of a kendra and a trikona, and the table does not follow him there. Where Parasara is silent, the table decides on its own. hora uses the table as printed, with no option for Parasara's lists.

## Read from any reference point {#reference}

The book reads functional nature from more than the lagna: it speaks of the yogakaraka from the Moon, and of a functional benefic in the dasamsa (D-10). hora reads the table for the rasi of the page's [reference point](/en/kb/bhava-significations) in each chart: the *Functional nature* column has one cell for the rasi chart and one for the varga chakra shown, each counted from the reference point's rasi in that chart (the lagna by default).

## Placement {#placement}

Where a graha sits matters as much as what it owns. A functional benefic in a kendra (which sustains) or a trikona (which brings prosperity) gives good results there. A functional malefic in those houses does not, unless it is very strong. In the 3rd and in the [dusthanas](/en/kb/bhava-categories#dusthana) (the 6th, 8th and 12th), a functional malefic does well, because it spoils the matters of those bad houses. The book gives no measure of "very strong", so hora does not compute these readings.

## Afflictions in Raaja yogas {#afflictions}

A [Raaja yoga](/en/kb/raaja-yogas#magnitude) gives less when a functional malefic afflicts its grahas (ch. 11 §11.7.2). On the chart page, each Raaja yoga row lists every functional malefic from the lagna of the chart read, other than the yoga's own grahas, that is in the rasi of one of them or aspects one by [graha drishti](/en/kb/aspects-and-argalas#graha-drishti), with how far apart they are in the rasi chart. hora names the afflictions and does not rate them.

## Luck beyond function {#luck}

Functional nature is not the whole story. A [yogada](/en/kb/baadhaka-and-yogada#yogada) brings good luck whatever its functional nature, and so do grahas that take part in important yogas.
