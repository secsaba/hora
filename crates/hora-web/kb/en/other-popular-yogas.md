# Other popular yogas

The book gathers here a long list of well-known combinations that belong to none of the earlier classes (§11.6). A dasa may bring some of a yoga's results even when not all its conditions are met, but for the yoga to be fully present every condition must hold and the planets taking part must be strong. The book gives no measure for that strength, so hora does not apply it to every yoga; it names "strong" to judge only where a rule asks for it. The general cautions that apply to every yoga are on the [yogas](/en/kb/yogas#caveats) page.

A few readings run through the whole list (ADR 0014):

- **Lords.** A bhava's lord is a lord in a yoga, as on the [yogas](/en/kb/yogas#readings) page. Where a rule asks for a lord in his own rasi and the house is Aquarius or Scorpio, the seven-planet co-lord, Saturn or Mars, at home there also serves.
- **Two grahas.** "Joins", "with" and "conjoined" relate two different grahas. A lord who turns out to be the graha he must join forms no yoga.
- **Kendras and trikonas.** The same rasi counts as a [kendra](/en/kb/bhava-categories#kendra) (quadrant) or a [trikona](/en/kb/bhava-categories#trikona) (trine) from a graha, but a graha is never in a kendra from himself.
- **A friend's or an enemy's rasi** is read by the [compound relationship](/en/kb/graha-relationships#compound) with the rasi's lord.

### Subha yoga {#subha}

Subha means auspicious.

**Rule:** the lagna holds benefics, or has subha kartari: benefics in both the 12th and the 2nd.

**Results:** eloquent; good-looking; of good character.

Kartari means a pair of scissors: the 12th and 2nd from any house close on it like blades. Benefics in both give subha (benefic) kartari, which helps that house's matters; malefics in both give paapa (malefic) kartari, which harms them. They are also called subha kartari yoga and paapa kartari yoga, and can be read for any house or graha. hora computes kartari only on the lagna, in these two yogas.

### Asubha yoga {#asubha}

**Rule:** the lagna holds malefics, or has paapa kartari: malefics in both the 12th and the 2nd.

**Results:** many desires; sinful; lives on the wealth of others.

### Gaja-Kesari yoga {#gaja-kesari}

**Rule:** Jupiter is in a kendra from the Moon; a benefic conjoins or aspects Jupiter; and Jupiter is neither debilitated, nor combust, nor in an enemy's rasi.

**Results:** famous; wealthy; intelligent; of great character; favoured by kings. Above all, a yoga of virtue and lasting fame.

Some authors also accept Jupiter in a kendra from the lagna instead of the Moon, and, if he is strong, the yoga without a benefic's conjunction or aspect. hora computes the book's rule only.

### Guru-Mangala yoga {#guru-mangala}

**Rule:** Jupiter and Mars are in the same rasi, or in the 7th from each other.

**Results:** righteous; energetic; his energies run along dharmic (righteous) paths.

### Amala yoga {#amala}

Amala means pure. The 10th shows one's conduct in society, and only benefics there make that conduct spotless.

**Rule:** only natural benefics occupy the 10th from the lagna or from the Moon.

**Results:** lasting fame; respected by kings; luxuries; virtuous; helps others.

### Parvata yoga {#parvata}

Parvata means a mountain.

**Rule:** the kendras hold only benefics, and the 7th and 8th are empty or hold only benefics. hora reads this as no malefic in any kendra, at least one benefic in one of them, and nothing but benefics in the 7th and 8th.

**Results:** fortunate; eloquent; famous; generous; easy-going; fond of humour.

### Kaahala yoga {#kaahala}

Kaahala: excessive, or mischievous.

**Rule:** the 4th lord and Jupiter are in kendras from each other, and the lagna lord is strong. Or else, the 4th lord is exalted or in his own rasi and the 10th lord joins him. When Jupiter is himself the 4th lord, the first form has no pair.

**Results:** strong; daring; crafty; at the head of a big army; lord of a handful of villages.

Some read "9th lord" instead of Jupiter in the first form. hora computes Jupiter.

### Chaamara yoga {#chaamara}

A chaamara is a plumed whisk that servants wave to cool a king: the trappings of power.

**Rule:** the lagna lord is exalted in a kendra and aspected by Jupiter; or two benefics are together in the 7th, 9th or 10th.

**Results:** a king, or one respected by kings; long-lived; scholarly; eloquent; learned in many arts.

### Sankha yoga {#sankha}

Sankha means a conch shell.

**Rule:** the lagna lord is strong and the 5th and 6th lords are in kendras from each other. Or else, the lagna lord and the 10th lord are together in a chara (movable) rasi and the 9th lord is strong. The second form needs two different grahas, so a Virgo or Pisces lagna never has it.

**Results:** wealth, a spouse and children; kind; pious; intelligent; long-lived.

### Bheri yoga {#bheri}

Bheri means a kettledrum.

**Rule:** the 9th lord is strong, and planets occupy the 1st, 2nd, 7th and 12th. Or else, the 9th lord is strong, and Jupiter, Venus and the lagna lord are in kendras from one another. If the lagna lord is Jupiter or Venus, the remaining pair is enough.

**Results:** wealth, a spouse and children; may be a king; fame and character; virtuous; religious; enjoys pleasures.

### Mridanga yoga {#mridanga}

The mridanga is a rich, elegant drum popular in south India.

**Rule:** planets in their own or exaltation rasis stand in kendras and trikonas, and the lagna lord is strong. hora counts at least one such planet. A lagna lord who is alone in that dignity does not form it by himself.

**Results:** a king or one of a king's standing; happy.

### Sreenaatha yoga {#sreenaatha}

Sreenaatha, lord of riches and plenty, is also a name of Vishnu.

**Rule:** the 7th lord is exalted in the 10th, and the 10th lord is with the 9th lord. Read to the letter, only a Sagittarius lagna allows it, its 7th lord Mercury being exalted in Virgo.

**Results:** a great king, Indra's equal; Indra rules the gods.

### Matsya yoga {#matsya}

Matsya means a fish.

**Rule:** benefics are in the lagna and the 9th, some planets are in the 5th, and malefics are in the [chaturasras](/en/kb/bhava-categories#chaturasra), the 4th and 8th. Each of these houses holds at least one.

**Results:** an astrologer or a seer; kindness, character and intelligence in person; strong; good-looking; famous; learned; a tapasvi (one given to austerity).

### Koorma yoga {#koorma}

Koorma means a tortoise.

**Rule:** benefics in their own, exaltation or friendly rasis occupy the 5th, 6th and 7th, and malefics in their own or exaltation rasis occupy the 1st, 3rd and 11th.

**Results:** a king; pious; of good character; happy; helpful; famous.

### Khadga yoga {#khadga}

Khadga means a sword.

**Rule:** the 2nd lord is in the 9th, the 9th lord in the 2nd, and the lagna lord in a kendra or a trikona.

**Results:** skilful; wealthy; learned; happy; fortunate; intelligent; grateful; mighty.

### Kusuma yoga {#kusuma}

Kusuma means a flower.

**Rule:** the lagna is in a sthira (fixed) rasi, Venus is in a kendra, the Moon is in a trikona with a benefic, and Saturn is in the 10th.

**Results:** a king or one of a king's standing; generous; pleasures and happiness; a leader of his community; character and scholarship.

### Kalaanidhi yoga {#kalaanidhi}

Kalaanidhi: a store of arts and accomplishments.

**Rule:** Jupiter is in the 2nd or the 5th, conjoined or aspected by Mercury and Venus.

**Results:** character; happiness; good health; wealth; learning; respected by kings.

### Kalpadruma yoga {#kalpadruma}

The kalpadruma is a wish-granting tree of heaven. The yoga is also called Paarijaata yoga, after a heavenly flower.

**Rule:** take four grahas: the lagna lord; his dispositor (the lord of the rasi he occupies); and the dispositor of that second graha, once in the rasi chart and once in the navamsa (D-9). All four must be in kendras, trikonas or exaltation rasis. hora checks the yoga in the rasi chart only, since it mixes two charts.

**Results:** a king; fond of war; very prosperous; principled; strong; kind. The book adds that "fond of war" is meant well: fighting wrongdoers is a king's duty.

Some authors simplify the rule so that one of the last two grahas in a kendra, trikona or exaltation rasi is enough. The book keeps Parasara's four, which makes the yoga rare, as it should be, and so does hora. The book's example is chart 9 (pp. 128–129): its lagna lord the Sun is in Aquarius, Saturn its lord is in Libra, Venus its lord, and Saturn's navamsa dispositor is Saturn himself. The Sun is in a kendra, Saturn exalted and Venus in a trikona, and the navamsa strengthens them further.

### Lagnaadhi yoga {#lagnaadhi}

Lagnaadhi is the [Adhi yoga](/en/kb/ravi-and-chandra-yogas#adhi) counted from the lagna; adhi means over or above.

**Rule:** benefics occupy the 7th and 8th from the lagna, and no malefic conjoins or aspects them. hora asks for two or more natural benefics there in any arrangement, a waxing Moon counting as one of them, none conjoined by a malefic or aspected by one with graha drishti; a Mercury who is kroora by his company counts as a malefic.

**Results:** a great person; learned; happy.

### Hari yoga {#hari}

Hari is a name of Vishnu, the sustainer. The 2nd, which holds one's food and money and is a trikona from the 10th, the house of work, is what keeps one going, so its lord plays Hari in the chart.

**Rule:** benefics occupy the 2nd, 12th and 8th counted from the 2nd lord. Between them these places must hold every natural benefic other than the 2nd lord himself.

**Results:** happy; learned; blessed with wealth and children.

### Hara yoga {#hara}

Hara is a name of Siva, the destroyer. Death belongs to the 7th, so its lord plays Siva in the chart; the 7th also signifies the genitals, and Siva is worshipped as a linga.

**Rule:** benefics occupy the 4th, 9th and 8th counted from the 7th lord, holding between them every natural benefic other than the 7th lord himself.

**Results:** happy; learned; blessed with wealth and children.

### Brahma yoga {#brahma}

Brahma is the creator. The lagna rules birth, so its lord stands for him.

**Rule:** benefics occupy the 4th, 10th and 11th counted from the lagna lord, holding between them every natural benefic other than the lagna lord himself. A second form: Jupiter is in a kendra from the 9th lord, Venus in a kendra from the 11th lord, and Mercury in a kendra from the lagna lord or the 10th lord. Since a graha is never in a kendra from himself, the second form fails when Jupiter is the 9th lord, Venus the 11th, or Mercury both the 1st and the 10th, so Aries, Cancer, Sagittarius and Virgo lagnas never have it.

**Results:** happy; learned; blessed with wealth and children.

Some authors join Hari, Hara and Brahma into one Hari-Hara-Brahma yoga. The three are also called the Trimurthi yogas, after the trinity of the creator Brahma, the sustainer Hari and the destroyer Siva.

### Vishnu yoga {#vishnu}

**Rule:** the 9th and 10th lords are in the 2nd, and so is the lord of the rasi that the 9th lord occupies in the navamsa. That navamsa dispositor must be a graha other than the 9th lord. Rasi chart only.

**Results:** fortunate; learned; long-lived; favoured by kings; a worshipper of Vishnu.

### Siva yoga {#siva}

**Rule:** the 5th lord is in the 9th, the 9th lord in the 10th and the 10th lord in the 5th.

**Results:** wise; virtuous; a conqueror; may be a commander of an army or a man of business.

### Trilochana yoga {#trilochana}

Trilochana, "three-eyed", is a name of Siva, whose third eye is hidden in his forehead.

**Rule:** the Sun, the Moon and Mars are in trikonas from one another; sharing a rasi counts.

**Results:** wealthy; intelligent; long-lived; overcomes enemies; reaches every goal with few hindrances.

### Gouri yoga {#gouri}

Gouri is a form of Parvati, Siva's wife, the very image of marital happiness and purity.

**Rule:** the lord of the rasi that the 10th lord occupies in the navamsa is exalted in the 10th, and the lagna lord joins him. Rasi chart only.

**Results:** of a respectable family; religious; virtuous; happiness from family.

### Chandikaa yoga {#chandikaa}

Chandikaa is a fierce form of Parvati who slays demons without mercy.

**Rule:** the lagna is in a sthira rasi aspected by the 6th lord, and the Sun joins the lords of the rasis that the 6th and 9th lords occupy in the navamsa. Rasi chart only.

**Results:** aggressive; generous; wealthy; famous; long-lived.

### Lakshmi yoga {#lakshmi}

Lakshmi, Vishnu's wife, is the goddess of prosperity.

**Rule:** the 9th lord is in his own or exaltation rasi, that rasi being a kendra from the lagna, and the lagna lord is strong.

**Results:** a king; good looks; character; wealth; many children; principled; famous.

### Saarada yoga {#saarada}

Saarada is a name of Saraswathi, the goddess of learning.

**Rule:** the 10th lord is in the 5th; Mercury is in a kendra; the Sun is strong in Leo; Mercury or Jupiter is in a trikona from the Moon; and Mars is in the 11th.

**Results:** wealth, a spouse and children; happy; learned; principled; favoured by kings; a tapasvi given to knowledge.

### Bhaarathi yoga {#bhaarathi}

Bhaarathi is another name of Saraswathi.

**Rule:** the lord of the rasi that the 2nd, 5th or 11th lord occupies in the navamsa is exalted and joins the 9th lord. Rasi chart only.

**Results:** a great scholar; intelligent; religious; good-looking; famous.

### Saraswathi yoga {#saraswathi}

Saraswathi is the goddess of learning.

**Rule:** Mercury, Jupiter and Venus each occupy a kendra, a trikona or the 2nd, not necessarily together, and Jupiter is in his own, a friend's or his exaltation rasi.

**Results:** very learned; skilful; intelligent; rich; famous; praised by all.

### Amsaavatara yoga {#amsaavatara}

Amsaavatara: one in whom part of the Lord is incarnate.

**Rule:** Jupiter, Venus and an exalted Saturn are in kendras.

**Results:** a king or one of a king's standing; learned; fond of pleasure; a spotless reputation.

### Devendra yoga {#devendra}

Devendra is the ruler of the gods.

**Rule:** the lagna is in a sthira rasi, the 2nd and 10th lords are in [parivartana](/en/kb/raaja-yogas#association) (exchange: each in the other's rasi), and so are the lagna and 11th lords.

**Results:** a leader of men; handsome; romantic; long-lived; famous.

### Indra yoga {#indra}

Indra is the ruler of the gods.

**Rule:** the 5th and 11th lords are in parivartana, and the Moon is in the 5th.

**Results:** a king; bold; famous; long-lived.

### Ravi yoga {#ravi}

Ravi means the Sun. This single yoga is not the class of Ravi yogas.

**Rule:** the Sun is in the 10th, and the 10th lord is in the 3rd with Saturn.

**Results:** learned; passionate; respected by kings.

### Bhaaskara yoga {#bhaaskara}

Bhaaskara, "the one with bright rays", is a name of the Sun.

**Rule:** the Moon is in the 12th from the Sun, Mercury in the 2nd from the Sun, and Jupiter in the 5th or 9th from the Moon.

**Results:** wealthy; valiant; aristocratic; learned in the sastras, astrology and music; a fine personality.

### Kulavardhana yoga {#kulavardhana}

Kula is a lineage or community, and a vardhana is one who increases and enriches it.

**Rule:** every planet occupies the 5th from the lagna, from the Moon or from the Sun.

**Results:** happy; wealthy; brings renown to his lineage and community; worthy heirs, generation after generation.

### Vasumati yoga {#vasumati}

Vasumati means the earth.

**Rule:** benefics occupy [upachayas](/en/kb/bhava-categories#upachaya), counted from the lagna, holding between them every natural benefic. For its full results, no malefic should be in an upachaya and the benefics there should be strong. hora names each malefic in an upachaya and each benefic's strength to judge.

**Results:** abundant wealth.

### Gandharva yoga {#gandharva}

The gandharvas are celestial beings, masters of song and the other fine arts.

**Rule:** the 10th lord is in a trikona from the 7th; the lagna lord is conjoined or aspected by Jupiter; the Sun is exalted and strong; and the Moon is in the 9th.

**Results:** gifted in the fine arts and renowned for them.

### Go yoga {#go}

Go means a cow.

**Rule:** Jupiter is strong in his [moolatrikona](/en/kb/dignity#moolatrikona), the 2nd lord is with Jupiter, and the lagna lord is exalted. hora takes the whole of Sagittarius as Jupiter's moolatrikona here.

**Results:** of a respectable family; wealthy; respected by all.

### Vidyut yoga {#vidyut}

Vidyut is lightning, or electricity.

**Rule:** the 11th lord is in deep exaltation, joins Venus, and the two are in a kendra from the lagna lord. Rasi chart only; hora checks the exaltation rasi and leaves the deep degree to judge.

**Results:** a king or one of a king's standing; wealthy; fond of pleasure; generous.

### Chapa yoga {#chapa}

Chapa means a bow. It is not the [Chaapa yoga](/en/kb/naabhasa-yogas#chaapa) of the Naabhasa yogas.

**Rule:** the 4th and 10th lords are in parivartana, and the lagna lord is exalted.

**Results:** works for a king; commands great wealth.

### Pushkala yoga {#pushkala}

Pushkala means abundant.

**Rule:** the lagna lord is with the Moon; the Moon's dispositor is in a kendra or in the rasi of an adhimitra (good friend) and aspects the lagna; and a planet is in the lagna.

**Results:** eloquent; wealthy; famous; respected by kings.

### Makuta yoga {#makuta}

Makuta means a crown.

**Rule:** Jupiter is in the 9th from the 9th lord, a benefic is in the 9th from Jupiter, and Saturn is in the 10th.

**Results:** a powerful leader of men; often in charge of unruly undertakings.

### Jaya yoga {#jaya}

Jaya means victorious.

**Rule:** the 10th lord is in deep exaltation and the 6th lord is debilitated. Rasi chart only; hora checks the exaltation rasi and leaves the deep degree to judge.

**Results:** happy; successful; overcomes enemies; long-lived.

### Harsha yoga {#harsha}

Harsha means joyous.

**Rule:** the 6th lord is in the 6th.

**Results:** happy; strong; good-natured; invincible.

### Sarala yoga {#sarala}

Sarala means straightforward.

**Rule:** the 8th lord is in the 8th.

**Results:** long-lived; fearless; learned; celebrated; prosperous; a terror to his enemies.

### Vimala yoga {#vimala}

Vimala means pure.

**Rule:** the 12th lord is in the 12th.

**Results:** noble; frugal; happy; independent.
