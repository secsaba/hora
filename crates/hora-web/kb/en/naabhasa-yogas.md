# Naabhasa yogas

Naabhasa yogas are classified celestial combinations: patterns of the whole sky rather than of one graha or one bhava (§11.5). Where other yogas give their results mainly in the dasas (periods) of the grahas and rasis that form them, Naabhasa yogas act in every dasa. There are four groups:

- **Aasraya yogas** (3): by the kind of rasi the planets occupy.
- **Dala yogas** (2): by the kendras benefics or malefics occupy.
- **Aakriti yogas** (20): by the shape the planets make in the chart.
- **Sankhya yogas** (7): by how many rasis the planets occupy.

"All the planets" here means the seven planets. hora counts Rahu and Ketu wherever a rule asks for a malefic, as in Sarpa, Vajra and Yava, though many authors leave them out of these yogas. The general cautions that apply to every yoga are on the [yogas](/en/kb/yogas#caveats) page.

## Aasraya yogas {#aasraya}

Aasraya means a dwelling or a refuge. These three ask whether every planet stands in one [kind of rasi](/en/kb/rasi-characteristics#chara-sthira-dwiswabhava): all in chara (movable), all in sthira (fixed) or all in dwiswabhava (dual) rasis.

### Rajju yoga {#rajju}

Rajju means a rope.

**Rule:** all the planets are in chara rasis and nowhere else.

**Results:** fond of travel; good-looking; prospers in foreign lands; cruel.

### Musala yoga {#musala}

Musala means a pestle.

**Rule:** all the planets are in sthira rasis and nowhere else.

**Results:** honour; wisdom; wealth; favoured by kings; famous; many children; a steady spirit.

### Nala yoga {#nala}

**Rule:** all the planets are in dwiswabhava rasis and nowhere else.

**Results:** a weak physique; gathers money; good-looking; helps relatives; skilful.

## Dala yogas {#dala}

Each of these needs three of the four [kendras](/en/kb/bhava-categories#kendra) (quadrants) from the lagna occupied by grahas of one nature. If a graha of the other nature also stands in any kendra, the yoga may not work well. hora then lists it to judge and says so.

### Maalaa yoga {#maalaa}

Maalaa means a garland.

**Rule:** natural benefics occupy three kendras. A lagna in Aries with Jupiter in Cancer, Venus in Capricorn and Mercury in Libra, for example.

**Results:** always happy; fine clothes, vehicles and luxuries; the friendship of many women.

### Sarpa yoga {#sarpa}

Sarpa means a serpent.

**Rule:** natural malefics occupy three kendras. A lagna in Scorpio with Mars in Taurus, Rahu in Leo and Ketu in Aquarius, for example.

**Results:** a very bad combination: miserable; unhappy; cruel; poor; dependent on others for food.

## Aakriti yogas {#aakriti}

Aakriti means a shape, and most of these yogas are named for the figure the planets draw in the chart. In each, all seven planets are in the places listed, and in those with fixed places every place holds at least one of them. Kamala and Vaapi ask only that the planets be within their places. More than one Aakriti yoga can be present in a chart.

### Gadaa yoga {#gadaa}

Gadaa means a mace.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy two kendras that follow one another, such as the 4th and 7th, or the 10th and 1st.

**Results:** wealth, gold and gems; performs yajnas (sacrificial rites); knows the sastras (sciences) and songs.

### Sakata yoga {#sakata}

Sakata means a cart.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the 1st and 7th from the lagna.

**Results:** suffers from diseases; ugly nails; foolish; without friends or family; earns a living by pulling carts.

### Vihanga yoga {#vihanga}

Vihanga means a bird. Some authors call it Vihaga yoga.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the 4th and 10th from the lagna.

**Results:** loves to roam; works as a messenger; fond of sex; shameless; quarrelsome.

### Sringaataka yoga {#sringaataka}

Sringaataka means a crossroads, among other meanings.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the [trikonas](/en/kb/bhava-categories#trikona) (trines) from the lagna: the 1st, 5th and 9th.

**Results:** happy; favoured by kings; a noble wife; hates women; wealthy.

### Hala yoga {#hala}

Hala means a plough.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy rasis in trine to one another, but not the trines from the lagna: the 2nd, 6th and 10th, or the 3rd, 7th and 11th, or the 4th, 8th and 12th.

**Results:** a farmer; eats a great deal; poor; abandoned by friends and relatives; unhappy and full of worry.

### Vajra yoga {#vajra}

Vajra means a diamond.

**Rule:** natural benefics occupy the 1st and 7th, and natural malefics the 4th and 10th.

**Results:** happy in the first and last parts of life; valiant; not lucky, yet without desires; given to fighting.

### Yava yoga {#yava}

Yava means a grain of barley, among other things.

**Rule:** natural malefics occupy the 1st and 7th, and natural benefics the 4th and 10th.

**Results:** keeps religious observances; happy in the middle part of life; wealth and good children; generous; firm of mind.

### Kamala yoga {#kamala}

Kamala means a lotus.

**Rule:** all the planets are in kendras from the lagna.

**Results:** a king; strong character; famous; long-lived; pure; does many good deeds.

### Vaapi yoga {#vaapi}

Vaapi means a pond, a tank or a well.

**Rule:** all the planets are in [panapharas](/en/kb/bhava-categories#panaphara) (succedent houses), or all are in [apoklimas](/en/kb/bhava-categories#apoklima) (cadent houses). A mix of the two does not count.

**Results:** a mind able to pile up wealth; every comfort; becomes a king.

### Yoopa yoga {#yoopa}

Yoopa means a pillar, in particular a sacrificial post.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th from the lagna.

**Results:** spiritual knowledge and knowledge of yajnas; his wife is always at his side; sattwa guna; keeps every religious observance.

### Sara yoga {#sara}

Sara means an arrow.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th from the lagna.

**Results:** makes arrows; in charge of prisons; a hunter; eats meat; tortures people.

### Sakti yoga {#sakti}

Sakti means energy, and is also the name of a mighty weapon.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the 7th, 8th, 9th and 10th from the lagna.

**Results:** unhappy; poor; unsuccessful; unworthy; lazy; long-lived; steadfast; a sharp mind in war.

### Danda yoga {#danda}

Danda means a rod of punishment.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the 10th, 11th, 12th and 1st from the lagna.

**Results:** loses wife and children; abandoned by his own people; unhappy; serves base people.

### Naukaa yoga {#naukaa}

Naukaa means a ship.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the seven rasis from the lagna onwards.

**Results:** earns from things connected with water; many desires; well known; wicked; harsh; miserly.

### Koota yoga {#koota}

Koota means a group, among several other meanings.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the seven rasis from the 4th onwards.

**Results:** a jailer; a liar; lives among hills and forts; poor; cruel.

### Chatra yoga {#chatra}

Chatra means an umbrella.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the seven rasis from the 7th onwards.

**Results:** helps his own people; kind; favoured by many kings; intelligent; happy in the first and last parts of life; long-lived.

### Chaapa yoga {#chaapa}

Chaapa means a bow. It is not the Chapa yoga of the [other popular yogas](/en/kb/other-popular-yogas#chapa).

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the seven rasis from the 10th onwards.

**Results:** a liar; a thief; a keeper of secrets; roams in forests; unlucky; happy in the middle part of life.

### Ardha Chandra yoga {#ardhachandra}

Ardha Chandra means a half Moon.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy seven rasis in a row starting from a panaphara or an apoklima.

**Results:** commander of an army; a fine physique; favoured by kings; strong; gems, gold and many ornaments.

### Chakra yoga {#chakra}

Chakra means a wheel; a chakravarti is an emperor.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th and 11th.

**Results:** a great emperor, at whose feet many kings lay their diamond-studded crowns.

### Samudra yoga {#samudra}

Samudra means the sea, and is also the god of the ocean, who keeps great wealth and many gems.

**Rule:** all the planets occupy the 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th and 12th.

**Results:** great wealth and many gems; luxuries; fond of people; lasting fortune and greatness; gentle by nature.

## Sankhya yogas {#sankhya}

Sankhya means a number. These yogas count how many different rasis the seven planets occupy between them, Rahu and Ketu left out. They apply only if no other Naabhasa yoga does, and they are the least important of the Naabhasa yogas (§11.5.4). hora lists one only when no other Naabhasa yoga is present or to judge.

So Gola is never listed: all seven planets in one rasi always give an Aasraya yoga as well. Likewise the book counts six rasis for Rama's chart (p. 123) and names Daama, but that chart also has Sarpa, so by the book's own proviso hora lists no Daama there (see [what was checked](/en/kb/yogas#checked)).

### Veenaa yoga {#veenaa}

The veenaa is a stringed instrument. Some authors call this Vallaki yoga.

**Rule:** the seven planets occupy exactly seven rasis.

**Results:** loves music, dance and song; many servants; wealthy; skilful; a leader of men.

### Daama yoga {#daama}

Daama means a wreath. Some authors call it Daamini yoga.

**Rule:** the seven planets occupy exactly six rasis.

**Results:** very rich; famous; many children; many gems; helps others.

### Paasa yoga {#paasa}

Paasa means a noose.

**Rule:** the seven planets occupy exactly five rasis.

**Results:** in danger of imprisonment; capable at work; talkative; many servants; lacking in character.

### Kedaara yoga {#kedaara}

Kedaara means a field.

**Rule:** the seven planets occupy exactly four rasis.

**Results:** a farmer; happy; wealthy; helpful to others.

### Soola yoga {#soola}

The soola is Siva's weapon, the trident.

**Rule:** the seven planets occupy exactly three rasis.

**Results:** sharp; lazy; violent; poor; shunned; valiant; wins honours in war.

### Yuga yoga {#yuga}

Yuga means a pair.

**Rule:** the seven planets occupy exactly two rasis.

**Results:** a heretic; poor; cast out by the world; irreligious; no happiness from children or from his mother.

### Gola yoga {#gola}

Gola means a sphere or a globe.

**Rule:** the seven planets are all in one rasi.

**Results:** strong; poor; dirty; uneducated; always sad.
