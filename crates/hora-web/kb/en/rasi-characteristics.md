# Rasi characteristics

A [rasi](/en/kb/rasi) (sign) is more than a 30° stretch of the zodiac. Each of the twelve carries a set of qualities, and a graha standing in a rasi, or a [bhava](/en/kb/bhava) (house) falling in it, takes on its colour. This page goes through those qualities one at a time, then gathers them rasi by rasi. Each table lists every value with the rasis that have it; a rasi's name takes you to its own section further down.

Many of the qualities repeat in a simple rhythm. Counting from Aries, the rasis alternate in twos, cycle in threes and cycle in fours, so a rasi's place in the count already says much about it.

## Limbs of Vishnu {#limb}

Tradition pictures the whole zodiac as the body of Vishnu, laid out from head to feet, Aries first and Pisces last. A person is a small copy of that body, so the same map serves a chart: to look into a part of the body, look at the rasi that stands for it.

{{table:limb}}

## Vishama and sama {#vishama-rasi}

The rasis alternate, starting with Aries: one vishama rasi (odd sign), also called oja, then one sama rasi (even sign), also called yugma. The odd ones count as male and the even ones as female. Some dasa systems run differently from the two kinds, and the split is also used when judging the sex of a child.

{{table:vishama-rasi}}

## Vishamapada and samapada {#vishamapada-rasi}

A second split into two halves of six, but in blocks rather than in alternation: three vishamapada rasis (odd-footed signs, also ojapada), then three samapada rasis (even-footed signs, also yugmapada), then three of each again. It too matters to some dasa systems.

{{table:vishamapada-rasi}}

## Chara, sthira, dwiswabhava {#chara-sthira-dwiswabhava}

How a rasi moves comes in a cycle of three. A chara rasi (movable sign) is restless and pushes for change; tradition places it under Brahma, the creator. A sthira rasi (fixed sign) holds on and resists change, under Shiva, the destroyer. A dwiswabhava rasi (dual sign) is sometimes one and sometimes the other, under Vishnu, the preserver.

{{table:chara-sthira-dwiswabhava}}

## Elements {#element}

Hindu philosophy builds everything, matter and mind alike, from five elements. Agni (fire) changes whatever it touches. Bhoo (earth) is solid and keeps its shape. Vaayu (air) shifts all the time. Jala (water) flows and takes the shape it is given. Aakaasa (ether) is the space the others fill. The first four go round the rasis in a cycle of four, fire first; ether is in every rasi and has no rasis of its own.

{{table:element}}

## Pitta, vaata, kapha {#pitta-vaata-kapha}

Ayurveda, India's traditional medicine, sees three natures in the body and in all else, each a blend of the elements. Pitta (bilious) is fire with water: it turns one thing into another and keeps the body warm. Vaata (windy) is air with ether: it carries whatever passes in and out, as the blood and the nerves carry their signals. Kapha (phlegmatic) is earth with water: it gives a system substance and holds it together, as the fluids that keep the joints supple do. Fiery rasis are pitta, earthy ones vaata and watery ones kapha; the airy rasis are a mixture.

{{table:pitta-vaata-kapha}}

## Guna {#guna}

Everything carries one of three gunas (qualities). Sattwa (purity) is clear, truthful and calm. Rajas (energy) is drive and passion, the urge to act and to want. Tamas (darkness) is heaviness, dullness and, at its worst, cruelty. A rasi with sattwa is called saattwik, one with rajas raajasik, one with tamas taamasik.

{{table:guna}}

## Directions {#direction}

Each element points to one direction: the fiery rasis to the east, the earthy ones to the south, the airy ones to the west and the watery ones to the north.

{{table:direction}}

## Colours {#colour}

Each rasi has a colour. Most are its own, but two pairs share one.

{{table:colour}}

## Divaa and nishaa {#divaa-rasi}

A divaa rasi (day sign) is strong in the daytime, a nishaa rasi (night sign) at night. Of the two rasis a graha owns, one is always of each kind. The Sun rules the divaa rasis and the Moon the nishaa ones.

{{table:divaa-rasi}}

## Seershodaya and prishthodaya {#seershodaya-rasi}

A seershodaya rasi (head-rising sign) comes over the horizon head first. A prishthodaya rasi (feet-rising sign) comes up the other way round; the word means rising with the back, though it is usually read as feet first. Pisces rises both ways. A graha in a head-rising rasi is said to show its results early in its dasa, one in a feet-rising rasi late.

{{table:seershodaya-rasi}}

## Varna {#varna}

Hindu society knew four varnas (classes): Brahmanas, who keep learning and the rites; Kshatriyas, who rule and fight; Vaisyas, who trade and provide; and Sudras, who do the work of the hands. Each is shown by the rasis of one element: the watery ones by Brahmanas, the fiery ones by Kshatriyas, the earthy ones by Vaisyas and the airy ones by Sudras.

{{table:varna}}

## What each rasi stands for {#indications}

Put the qualities together and each rasi takes on a character of its own. It speaks of a part of the body, of kinds of places, of a temperament and of a look. A reading draws on whichever matters: a rasi in a chart can describe a person's body, the places in their life or the mood of a matter. Below each sketch, its qualities from the tables above.

### Aries (Mesha) {#aries}

Aries opens the zodiac, and everything about it is a beginning. Movable fire with rajas, it acts first and thinks later: courageous, eager to lead, impatient. It governs the head and tends to give a wiry, energetic frame. Of places, it favours wild, unsettled country over towns.

{{characteristics:aries}}

### Taurus (Vrishabha) {#taurus}

Fixed earth with rajas, Taurus holds on to what it has and enjoys it: loyal, steady, fond of comfort and fine things, unwilling to be hurried. It governs the face and lends good looks and a sturdy build. Its places are open, level country and rooms where people dine and take their ease.

{{characteristics:taurus}}

### Gemini (Mithuna) {#gemini}

Dual air with tamas, Gemini lives by exchange: curious, learned and good-humoured, at home with words in every form, from conversation to the news. It governs the arms and the chest and gives a tall, well-made frame. Its places are those of study and teaching, and gardens.

{{characteristics:gemini}}

### Cancer (Karkataka) {#cancer}

Movable water with sattwa, Cancer feels everything and clings to those it loves; it nurtures as a mother does and is easily hurt. It governs the heart and the breast and gives a small, appealing figure. Its places lie by water, and at home around the hearth.

{{characteristics:cancer}}

### Leo (Simha) {#leo}

Fixed fire with sattwa, Leo carries itself like royalty: proud and in command, and at its worst arrogant. It governs the belly and the digestion and gives a lean frame of a hot, dry make. Its places are rugged and remote, and also the seats of power.

{{characteristics:leo}}

### Virgo (Kanya) {#virgo}

Dual earth with tamas, Virgo lives in its head: sharp, articulate and tactful, while the body may be frail and the nerves taut. It governs the hip and the lower belly. Its places are cultivated land and wherever books are kept.

{{characteristics:virgo}}

### Libra (Thula) {#libra}

Movable air with rajas, Libra is the rasi of the scales and of trade: balanced, sensible and a ready talker. It governs the body below the navel. Its places are where business is done and where people go for pleasure.

{{characteristics:libra}}

### Scorpio (Vrischika) {#scorpio}

Fixed water with rajas, Scorpio runs deep and hidden: intense, secretive, drawn to what is concealed, and touchy. It governs the private parts and gives a compact frame and a penetrating gaze. Its places are below ground or out of sight.

{{characteristics:scorpio}}

### Sagittarius (Dhanus) {#sagittarius}

Dual fire with sattwa, Sagittarius is honest and good-natured, at ease with authority and the law, yet fond of a gamble. It governs the thighs and gives a strong, upright frame. Its places are those of government.

{{characteristics:sagittarius}}

### Capricorn (Makara) {#capricorn}

Movable earth with tamas, Capricorn plans and persists: practical, cautious, patient and reserved, a careful organiser. It governs the knees and gives a slender frame. Its places are low, wet and overgrown.

{{characteristics:capricorn}}

### Aquarius (Kumbha) {#aquarius}

Fixed air with tamas, Aquarius is a steady, uncomplaining worker, honest and drawn to philosophy and to giving. It governs the ankles and gives a tall, rawboned frame. Its places are near water.

{{characteristics:aquarius}}

### Pisces (Meena) {#pisces}

Dual water with sattwa, Pisces closes the zodiac: gentle, emotional and honest, guided by intuition more than resolve, and fond of ease. It governs the feet and gives a short, rounded frame. Its places are the sea, and places of retreat or confinement.

{{characteristics:pisces}}
