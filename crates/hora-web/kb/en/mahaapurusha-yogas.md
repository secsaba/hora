# Mahaapurusha yogas

Pancha means five and mahaapurusha a great person: the Pancha Mahaapurusha yogas describe five kinds of great person (§11.4). Each is tied to one of the five elements, the pancha bhootas or pancha tattvas, of which the world is made, and to the planet that stands for it: Mars for fire, Mercury for earth, Saturn for air, Venus for water and Jupiter for ether. The element shapes the person's character.

All five share one rule: the planet is in its [own rasi](/en/kb/dignity#own-rasi) or its [uchcha](/en/kb/dignity#uchcha) (exaltation) rasi, and in a [kendra](/en/kb/bhava-categories#kendra) (quadrant), the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th, from the lagna. They are counted from the lagna, never from the Moon, and apply mainly in the rasi chart. hora checks them in the rasi chart and in the varga chakra shown. The general cautions that apply to every yoga are on the [yogas](/en/kb/yogas#caveats) page.

### Ruchaka yoga {#ruchaka}

**Rule:** Mars in Aries, Scorpio or Capricorn, in a kendra from the lagna. A Libra lagna with Mars in Aries, for example.

**Results:** a great person of fiery nature; full of enthusiasm; a born leader; loves to fight wars and overcomes enemies; discerning; devoted to the learned; versed in the occult sciences; of refined taste; always successful.

### Bhadra yoga {#bhadra}

**Rule:** Mercury in Gemini or Virgo, in a kendra from the lagna. A Gemini lagna with Mercury in Virgo, for example.

**Results:** a great person of earthy nature; lion-like; learned in every respect; well built; a deep voice; sattwa guna (the quality of purity); knows yoga well; shares his wealth with the relatives, friends and family always around him; clean in everything; very orderly; independent in spirit; religious.

### Sasa yoga {#sasa}

Sasa means a hare.

**Rule:** Saturn in Capricorn, Aquarius or Libra, in a kendra from the lagna. A Capricorn lagna with Saturn in Libra, for example.

**Results:** a great person of airy nature; hare-like; wise; fond of wandering; at home in forests, mountains and forts; brave; of slender build; knows the weaknesses of others; lively but somewhat wavering; generous.

### Maalavya yoga {#maalavya}

**Rule:** Venus in Taurus, Libra or Pisces, in a kendra from the lagna. A Gemini lagna with Venus in Pisces, for example.

**Results:** a great person of watery nature; a glow like moonlight; enjoys tasty food; luxuries; excellent health; versed in the arts.

### Hamsa yoga {#hamsa}

Hamsa means a swan. The book's definition calls this yoga "Ruchaka" by a slip; it is Hamsa.

**Rule:** Jupiter in Sagittarius, Pisces or Cancer, in a kendra from the lagna. A Sagittarius lagna with Jupiter in Pisces, for example.

**Results:** a great person of ethereal nature; swan-like; spiritual strength and purity; respected by all; very passionate; becomes a king; every comfort; enjoys life to the full; a clever talker with fine speech.
